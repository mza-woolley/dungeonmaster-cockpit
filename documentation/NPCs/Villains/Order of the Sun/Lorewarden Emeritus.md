# Lorewarden Emeritus

**Type:** DOL Leadership — The Order of the Sun  
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

The Lorewarden Emeritus is the senior archival and doctrinal authority within the Division of Lumination. He operates out of the Lumination Archive in Solvetra Middle and holds significant influence over the Order of the Sun's operations across the city.

---

## Known Actions

- Gave **Plumbodian** a quest during Session 5 — details unrecorded; Plumbodian was reminded of it by note in Session 6
- **Fenrik** delivered a sword to him from the Blacksmith in Session 5 — was asked to follow up on Wizzleforth's involvement
- Signed the **Lumina Elixir distribution map** as *L.E.* — planned rollout across Ars Lapis
- Holds **Key 4** to the Solvetra Vault — the only key not readily accessible to the party
- Was spotted at the Solvetra Vault during Kaelen's stakeout (Session 7)

---

## Known Capabilities / Resources

- Controls the Lumination Archive
- Has authority over the Order of the Sun operatives stationed at the Solvetra Vault
- Connected to the Lumina Lab operation via the distribution map signature
