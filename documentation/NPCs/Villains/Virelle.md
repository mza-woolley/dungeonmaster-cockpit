# Virelle

**Type:** Head of the Order of the Sun (the DIL is the Order's administrative front)
**Role:** Elaris's mother; the hand behind the Armada of Glimmerdeep
**Status:** Active - whereabouts unknown to the party

---

## Overview

Virelle was theologically touched by **Sibling Thorne** (the Vassal of Light, ⅓ of Tre Illium) and given light powers meant to pass to her first-born - some leaked through to her directly in the touching. What should have been a single act of grace became the defining fact of her life. She didn't treat it as a gift to be stewarded. She treated it as proof - proof that ascension was possible, and that she was the one meant for it.

She sits at the very top of the Division of Lumination and the Order of the Sun, nominally - but she isn't running it. She stopped actively steering the Order a long time ago, once it became clear the institution itself was never the point. Ruling La'Choix was a side effect of her rise, not the goal. The goal is **light-godhood** - full ascension beyond what Sibling Thorne's touch gave her - and everything resembling "leadership" from her now is absentee, hands-off, do-whatever-you-want-with-it. The Order runs itself on doctrine, precedent, and inertia, not on her direct instruction.

That's *why* the DOL feels the way the party has experienced it - inconsistent, personally-driven, every operator running their own angle. The **Lorewarden Emeritus** answers to her in title only; his knowledge-hoarding, his own bargains, his personal accumulation of power exist precisely *because* the top of the chain never checks in. Valerius freelancing with hired thugs, the Order's soft-power comfort-control in places like Ars Pontaris, sincere true-believers like Brother Calis and Priestess Aurel Liss carrying doctrine that no one at the top is actively directing anymore - all of it is downstream of an authority who left the building. The party has been fighting her infrastructure for nine sessions without knowing whose it is, or that no one's actually driving it.

---

## The Descent

Virelle didn't start monstrous. She started devout - composed, radiant, the kind of certainty that reads as serenity rather than fanaticism. That's who Elaris remembers: a mother who kept a home shrine, who spoke of "the Order" the way other people speak of family tradition, who never raised her voice because she never needed to.

Somewhere along the way, "the Light" stopped being a faith and became a mission with no ceiling. Each escalation was justified by the one before it - until there was no line left she wouldn't cross to get closer to it.

- **She had a daughter and left her.** Elaris was raised believing her power came from her mother's "divine talent." It didn't - it came from Sibling Thorne, passing through Virelle like water through a pipe. Virelle knew this. She let her daughter grow up mistaking inheritance for a gift, and never corrected it.
- **She stole from - and erased - a man to get what she needed.** She took the real Orb from Plumbodian the 4th and sank the **Armada of Glimmerdeep** to cover the theft - an entire fleet, crews included, burned in a single act of light so that no one would ask where the Orb went. The trauma of the loss triggered his reincarnation as Plumbodian the 5th. She then implanted false memories in him, convincing him his family died at sea and that the powerful fake orb he now carries is the real one. He has no idea he was collateral in someone else's ambition, not a target of anything personal.
- **She burned every bridge that tied her to another living person.** A daughter she abandoned to a false understanding of herself. A man whose entire family history she overwrote. A fleet of nameless sailors sacrificed as a cover story. None of it was cruelty for its own sake - every act served "the Light" - which is exactly the problem. She has not once treated a person as anything other than a variable in the pursuit.

What's left of Virelle now isn't a woman with a cause. It's a cause that used to be a woman - and she's not building anything for anyone but herself. The Order, the Lorewarden, the guards, Solvetra's quiet control - none of that is her legacy project. It's scaffolding she stopped needing the moment it stood on its own. She's not interested in ruling. She's interested in what comes after ruling stops mattering.

## Known Capabilities

- Holds nominal supreme authority over the Division of Lumination and the Order of the Sun - rarely exercised, never checked
- Channels Sibling Thorne's imbued light directly - powers well beyond what a normal recipient of theological touch would carry
- Capable of sinking a fleet in a single burst of solar/light-based destruction (the Armada of Glimmerdeep)
- Capable of implanting false, deeply convincing memories (demonstrated on Plumbodian the 4th/5th)
- Currently holds the **real Orb** - attuned to the gate of Luxfracta, the key piece needed to activate the passthrough to the Realm of Shadowsong
- Could move the entire Order's resources - finances, personnel, doctrine - if she ever chose to. She doesn't. That's the point.

---

## What the Party Doesn't Know

- That Virelle exists as a person at all, let alone that she sits above the Lorewarden Emeritus at the top of the entire DOL/OOTS structure
- That the DOL's inconsistency - Valerius freelancing, the Lorewarden hoarding his own power, nobody at the top actually steering - isn't dysfunction. It's an empty throne.
- Where Virelle is now, or what she's doing with the Orb
- That her goal was never rule - she's chasing **light-godhood**, full ascension beyond what Sibling Thorne's touch gave her. The Order was a byproduct, not the plan. Exactly what "godhood" would look like achieved, or what the Orb/Luxfracta actually does for her, is TBD - way down the line.
- That Elaris's patron is Sibling Thorne, not her mother - Virelle either doesn't know this herself, or has known all along and let Elaris believe otherwise
- That the "family lost at sea" Plumbodian mourns never actually existed the way he remembers it
- Whether Virelle sees herself as a monster, a martyr, or simply correct
