# Zatharial

**Type:** Devil  
**Status:** Active — contract in play

---

## Overview

A devil who approached Adrion in Solvetra West (Session 4), offering to teleport him to the party in exchange for signing a contract. Adrion signed without reading it.

## The Contract

The contract grants Zatharial the right to claim **Adrion's soul and the party's souls** upon Adrion's death. The terms were never read aloud or explained before signing.

## Zatharial's Interest

Has a stated vested interest in the party — specifically, he believes they are the ones destined to defeat the Light Vassal. His interference is calculated, not random. He is flagged as a future force to exploit division within the party, using the contract as leverage against Adrion and potentially the group.

## Notes

- Can be used to remind Adrion of the contract terms at a dramatically appropriate moment
- The party may not all be aware of the full terms
- His involvement is not tied to the DOL or Shadowbringers — he operates independently
