# Zatharial

**Type:** Devil  
**Status:** Active — contract still intact, tearing planned for Session 8

---

## Overview

A devil who approached Adrion in Solvetra West (Session 4), offering to teleport him to the party in exchange for signing a contract. Adrion signed without reading it. The contract is **still active** — Zatharial has not yet appeared before the party regarding it. That confrontation, where he theatrically tears the contract in front of them, is planned for **Session 8**.

Inspiration: Raphael from Baldur's Gate 3. Charming, theatrical, unhurried. He doesn't threaten — he presents options. He always seems to be doing the party a favour, even when he isn't. He finds the whole situation delightful.

---

## The Contract (Active — Currently Bound)

The contract grants Zatharial the right to claim **the party's souls** upon Adrion's death. With Adrion gone, the souls are currently bound and Zatharial has every right to collect — but hasn't yet acted. In Session 8, he's planned to appear and tear it up publicly — a power move, not a concession. He wants the party to know he *chose* to let them go. For now.

This runs in parallel with [Lucien Morvault](Lucien%20Morvault.md)'s active claim on the party — two live soul/collateral claims simultaneously through Session 7.

---

## Zatharial's Interest

He believes the party are destined to defeat the Light Vassal. His interest in them is genuine but entirely self-serving — he wants the Light Vassal gone, and these are the people who can do it. He will help, hinder, and manipulate as it suits him, always with a smile.

He does not work for the DOL or the Shadowbringers. He operates on a longer timeline than either.

---

## How to Play Him

- Always composed, always a step ahead — or performing like he is
- Never raises his voice; the quieter he gets, the more dangerous he is
- Frames everything as a gift or an opportunity: *"I'm not asking for much — consider it a favour between friends"*
- Session 8 plan: tear up the contract in front of the party not to free them, but to remind them he *could have* collected and chose not to — they owe him something now, even though nothing more was signed
- Will reappear at dramatically inconvenient moments with a new proposition

---

- His involvement is not tied to the DOL or Shadowbringers — he operates independently
