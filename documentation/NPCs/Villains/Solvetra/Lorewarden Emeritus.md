# Lorewarden Emeritus

**Type:** DOL Leadership - The Order of the Sun  
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

The Lorewarden Emeritus is the senior archival and doctrinal authority within the Division of Lumination. He operates out of the Lumination Archive in Solvetra Middle and holds significant influence over the Order of the Sun's operations across the city.

---

## Known Actions

- Gave **Plumbodian** a quest during Session 5 (the Living Manuscript) - reminded by note in Session 6, again by letter in Session 9, and resolved in person the same session. See [Completed Quests - Solvetra Middle](../../../../Quests/Completed/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Middle/Completed%20Quests.md).
- **Fenrik** delivered a sword to him from the Blacksmith in Session 5 - was asked to follow up on Wizzleforth's involvement
- Signed the **Lumina Elixir distribution map** as *L.E.* - planned rollout across Ars Lapis
- Holds **Key 4** to the Solvetra Vault - the only key not readily accessible to the party
- May be spotted at the Solvetra Vault during Kaelen's stakeout - planned for Session 7, not yet played

---

## Known Capabilities / Resources

- Controls the Lumination Archive
- Has authority over the Order of the Sun operatives stationed at the Solvetra Vault
- Connected to the Lumina Lab operation via the distribution map signature

## Notes

- The letter found at the Missing Guest quest (The Final Rest) was addressed to *L.E.* - *"The Shadowbringers, my lord - I've been had. I've been found. I will die a worthy death. Hail the Order of the Sun!"* This predates the Lumina Lab raid (Session 6/7) - he already had a source watching the Shadowbringers before any of the party's recent activity. The spy was found and killed for it - the network was thinned, not fully burned.
- His quest to the party (Living Manuscript) involves recovering a forbidden volume - reward is an amulet with 1 × L2 Spell Slot for Wizards
- **The conversation(s) between him and the party in Session 5 are not fully documented - reconstruct from memory before next session**
- **Session 9:** Sent Plumbodian a letter chasing an update on the Living Manuscript. P.S.: *"I heard old Morvault wants your money. I still need your help. Perhaps we can sort something out."* - first indication he's aware of (and possibly willing to leverage) the party's debt to [Lucien Morvault](../Solvetra%20Intermellum/Lucien%20Morvault.md).
- **Session 9, in person:** the party (Kaelen, Wizzleforth, Plumbodian - joined by Fenrik, Frah'nk, Elaris) met him at his office (reached via the Solvetra Windmill) to return the manuscript. Plumbodian handed it over and was gifted **The Division's Necklace** (amulet, +1 L2 Spell Slot). In the same meeting he: chastised Elaris over her company and pointedly asked whether Virelle is aware of it; pushed on Frah'nk about the Greyveil Suppression without getting a rise out of him; threatened Kaelen after he tried to be smart with him; invited Plumbodian to come talk further about his orb; and reiterated - now face to face - that he's aware of the party's debt to Lucien Morvault and may (or may not) help. Open question: is this genuine help, or another string attached alongside Key 4? He also let slip that he knows about **Headguard Valerius** and that the party did him a favour - worth clarifying next session whether that means Valerius's death specifically, or something else. See [Solvetra Headguard Replacement](../../../In%20Progress/Solvetra%20Headguard%20Replacement.md).
- His read on the Lumina Lab's loss is "the Shadowbringers struck, as expected" - not "the PCs did this." The total wipeout of Lab personnel is more thorough than he'd expect from the Shadowbringers, an open question he's choosing not to push on (risks exposing his source, or the Lab's Worg-abuse origins). His awareness of the party's specific involvement is unconfirmed.
