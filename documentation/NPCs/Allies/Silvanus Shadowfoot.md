# Silvanus Shadowfoot

**Race/Class:** Oathbreaker Paladin (formerly sworn to the Oath of the Lumination / Order of the Sun's "Dawnbringers")  
**Role:** Silas' father  
**Where Met:** Upstairs at the Smuggler's Den (if PCs go looking for Silas)

Hulking but only 5'0", quite old, walks with a metal leg lost in a thieving ambush that also took his wife (Silas' mother) and his daughter (Silas' sister, kidnapped, never found dead or alive). He murdered the thieves in cold blood afterward and now walks the Path of the Shadowbringer, hoping one day to collapse the Dawnbringers' organisation. Carries clear sadness when the topic comes up.

---

## The Greyveil Suppression

Silvanus' faith in the Order was already fractured long before the ambush. As a Dawnbringer, he was conscripted into **The Greyveil Suppression** — the OOTS-ordered clearing of what is now Solvetra East. The district was a thriving common area at the time. The residents were unarmed. Silvanus and the Dawnbringers were told it was civic restructuring. What it was, was a forced removal carried out at swordpoint against civilians who had done nothing except live somewhere the Order wanted.

He followed orders. He has never forgiven himself for it.

The ambush that took his family came later — but it landed on ground already broken. The Order had shown him what it was at Greyveil. The ambush just removed any reason left to stay.

---

## Frah'nk Frapper

Silvanus met **Frah'nk Frapper** at Greyveil — both conscripted, both from hedge knight families with Order ties, both disgusted by what they were ordered to do. The shared experience of that day is the foundation of a long friendship. Frah'nk eventually found his way to the Shadowbringers through his own path; their friendship deepened when they crossed paths again within the organisation.

Silvanus recently wrote to Frah'nk asking him to find the party — the group that has fallen in around Silas. He trusts Frank to assess them honestly and watch his son's back without making it obvious that's what he's doing.
