# Silvanus Shadowfoot

**Race/Class:** Oathbreaker Paladin (formerly sworn to the Oath of the Lumination / Order of the Sun's "Dawnbringers")
**Role:** Silas' father
**Where Met:** Upstairs at the Smuggler's Den (if PCs go looking for Silas)

Hulking but only 5'0", quite old, walks with a metal leg lost in a thieving ambush that also took his wife (Silas' mother) and his daughter (Silas' sister, kidnapped, never found dead or alive). The tragedy broke his faith in the Division/Order — he murdered the thieves in cold blood and now walks the Path of the Shadowbringer, hoping one day to collapse the Dawnbringers' organisation. Carries clear sadness when the topic comes up.
