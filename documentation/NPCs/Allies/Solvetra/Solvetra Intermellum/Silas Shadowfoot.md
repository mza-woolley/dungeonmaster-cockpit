# Silas Shadowfoot

**Race:** Halfling (Male)
**Role:** Leader, Shadowbringer Deliverers - Shadowbringers "main quest" giver
**Status:** Active companion / party ally
**Where Met:** Lightstation Solvetra West (Session 5), reconvenes at The Final Rest, Solvetra Middle
**Father:** [Silvanus Shadowfoot](Silvanus%20Shadowfoot.md)

Short, brown-haired halfling in grey/black scout gear - travels light and nimble, bow on his back, quiver at the hip. Calm and purposeful; commanding but measured - gives the party direction without overstepping. Carries the weight of his father's cause without his father's bitterness.

---

## Background

Leader of the **Shadowbringer Deliverers**, the elite operational arm of the Shadowbringers. Introduced the party to the rebellion's cause and has been their primary contact since Session 5.

His father, **Silvanus Shadowfoot**, is a former Dawnbringer who broke his oath and now walks the Path of the Shadowbringer. The family were devastated by a thieving ambush that took Silvanus' leg, his wife, and his daughter (Silas' sister) - who was never found. See [Silas' Sister](../../../../Story%20Threads/Friends/Silas'%20Sister.md).

---

## Role in the Plot

- Recruited the party after Shadowbringer spies saw what happened at Solvetra Meadows and reported back - he thinks they could be the force needed to turn the tide against the Order.
- Stresses the party needs to build a reputation around town and stay in good standing before taking on the Order directly.
- Lore-dumps on the Order of the Sun (knows *who* some of them are, not *why* they do what they do).
- Sent the party to investigate **[Lumina Lab](../../../../Locations/Dungeons,%20Ruins%20&%20Others/Lumina%20Lab.md)** - strange transactions, and a goblin sighting (Session 6).
- Personally escorted the party into Lumina Lab, arranged a daytime distraction via two unnamed companions, and fought alongside them (Goblin Warriors, Boss Dragg).
- After Valerius fell, explained "The Order" to the party.
- Received the letter from [Lucien Morvault](../../../Villains/Solvetra/Lucien%20Morvault.md) addressed to Adrion; aware the party is in debt.
- Introduced [Du'geshk Maharjuuj](Du'geshk%20Maharjuuj.md) and kicked off the Solvetra Vault heist operation - see [The Big One](../../../../Quests/Active/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/The%20Big%20One.md) (Session 7).
