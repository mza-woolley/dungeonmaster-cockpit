# Silas Shadowfoot

**Race:** Halfling
**Role:** Shadowbringers "main quest" giver — son of [Silvanus Shadowfoot](Silvanus%20Shadowfoot.md)
**Where Met:** Lightstation Solvetra West (Session 5), reconvenes at The Final Rest, Solvetra Middle

Short, brown-haired halfling in grey/black scout gear — travels light and nimble, bow on his back, quiver at the hip. Calm and purposeful; carries the weight of his father's cause without his father's bitterness.

---

## Role in the Plot

- Tells the party that Shadowbringer spies saw what happened at Solvetra Meadows and reported back — he thinks the party could be the force needed to turn the tide against the Order.
- Stresses the party needs to build a reputation around town and stay in good standing before taking on the Order directly.
- Lore-dumps on the Order of the Sun (knows *who* some of them are, not *why* they do what they do).
- Sends the party to investigate **Lumina Lab** — strange transactions, and a goblin sighting (Session 6).
- Personally escorts the party into Lumina Lab, arranges a daytime distraction via two unnamed companions, and fights alongside them (Goblin Warriors, Boss Dragg).
- After Valerius falls, explains "The Order" to the party.

---

## Notes

- Father is [Silvanus Shadowfoot](Silvanus%20Shadowfoot.md) — Silvanus asked Frah'nk Frapper to watch Silas' back without making it obvious.
- Recurring quest-giver for the Shadowbringers' main thread — likely the party's primary contact going into Solvetra Intermellum.
