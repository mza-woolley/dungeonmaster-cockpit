# Grakh

**Race/Class:** Human, Barkeeper
**Role:** Runs the bar at the Smuggler's Den
**Where Met:** Smuggler's Den, Solvetra Intermellum

Short, scraggly human with a thick Aussie bogan accent — completely at odds with the grim, shadowy vibe of the Den itself. Keeps the party fed and watered, and is trusted enough by the Shadowbringers to hold notes, messages, and errands on their behalf.
