# Garrik Windtaker

**Role:** Owner/operator, Whistlewind Rise (windmill)
**Where Met:** Whistlewind Rise, Solvetra Meadows - Session 2/3

Grateful, generous, and a little overwhelmed - three DOL priests had been quietly replacing the windmill's lunar symbols before the party dealt with them. Garrik rewarded the party with a mass supply of food once the gear was fixed and the bodies were out of sight.

---

## Quest Tie-In

Resolved - DOL priests removed, windmill gear fixed, lunar symbols intact. Garrik is friendly ground if the party passes back through the Meadows.
