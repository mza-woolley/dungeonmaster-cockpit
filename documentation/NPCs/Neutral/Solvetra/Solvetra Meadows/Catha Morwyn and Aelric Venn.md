# Catha Morwyn & Aelric Venn

**Role:** Innhand (Catha) and Innkeep (Aelric), The Pale Chalice
**Where Met:** The Pale Chalice, Solvetra Meadows - Session 1

Catha is the put-upon innhand with a grudge against Titus; Aelric runs the place with weary patience for the regulars' nonsense. The Pale Chalice is the party's unofficial home base in the Meadows - expect them to be the first familiar faces on any return visit.

---

## Quest Tie-In

Catha's grievance against **Titus** ("Humiliate Titus") was the hook that kicked off Session 1 - the party tied Titus to a tree and interrogated him on her behalf. Resolved; see [Completed Quests](../../../../Quests/Completed/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Meadows/Completed%20Quests.md).
