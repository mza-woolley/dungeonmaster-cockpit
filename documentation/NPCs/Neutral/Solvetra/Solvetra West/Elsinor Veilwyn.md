# Elsinor Veilwyn

**Role:** Keeper, Fountain of Dreams
**Where Met:** Fountain of Dreams, Solvetra West

Dreamy and ornamental, much like the fountain itself — speaks in soft riddles about wishes and reflections, the kind of person who's been quietly watching the square for years and noticed everything.
