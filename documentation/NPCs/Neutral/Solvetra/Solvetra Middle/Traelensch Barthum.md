# Traelensch Barthum

**Race:** Elf
**Role:** Bank Teller (leftmost desk), the inside contact/mole
**Where Met:** Solvetra Vault, Level 1

Eloquent older gentleman, kind to players' requests, claims to be new and constantly defers to his supervisor Kaeren. He is, in fact, Du'geshk's "inside contact" - Rissa Varn can be paid for the information identifying him as such. Players can strongarm him by revealing they know he's an insider, but he may try to extort cash from them in turn or threaten to hit the panic button if pushed too hard.
