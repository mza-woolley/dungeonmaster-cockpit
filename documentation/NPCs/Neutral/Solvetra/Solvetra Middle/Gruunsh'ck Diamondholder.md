# Gruunsh'ck Diamondholder

**Race:** Goblin
**Role:** Bank Teller (rightmost desk)
**Where Met:** Solvetra Vault, Level 1

Nerdy little goblin who "akshually"s the players while walking them through the bureaucratic Form 32B pipeline: Form 1A (chosen class) → Form 1475L (goals/ambitions) → Form 69X (sexual activity in last 45 days) → Form 32B (Bank Teller test - what would you do if someone tried to steal all the gold; acceptable answers range from immediate surrender to hitting the panic button).

## Session 8

Reviewed Plumbodian's stack of intern bond paperwork, questioned the "X" signature Plumbodian used to sign off on it, and accepted it after Plumbodian talked him around (Deception 16). Sent Plumbodian to the Dye House in Solvetra Intermellum to see Callen Droth, and dropped lore that Solvetra Vault currently isn't letting citizens withdraw their money.

Later, when Plumbodian returned with the bank notes from the Dye House, Gruunsh'ck clarified that no one is addressing Vanya Windros's continued absence - bonds keep landing on her desk and getting processed anyway, with no one raising the alarm.
