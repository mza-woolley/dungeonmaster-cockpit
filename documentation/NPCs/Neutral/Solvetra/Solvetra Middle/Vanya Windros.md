# Vanya Windros

**Role:** Bank Manager, Solvetra Vault (found - Session 12)
**Where Met:** Referenced throughout - never directly encountered (her office, B1)

Disappeared without a trace mid-shift; her office shows no sign of struggle. Without her, the bank vault codes can't be retrieved and the heist stalls - the vault has 3 simultaneous, blast-resistant locks requiring keys that no one has seen all in one place.

Her locked office (DC 17 lock) holds a golden key on the desk and several odd details: the room is unnaturally clean with fresh fruit out, a pristine unused fireplace, a pamphlet about "Arcane Vault Guards" protocol, and (on a higher check) a draft and dirt footprints at the chimney base with a hidden note listing spells: Message, Invisibility, Disguise Self, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, Detect Object - suggesting she may have escaped or been taken via the chimney using magic.

---

## Session 8

Gruunsh'ck confirmed to Plumbodian that no one on staff is formally addressing Vanya's continued absence - bonds keep landing on her desk and getting processed regardless, with no one raising it. Reads as either bureaucratic negligence or a deliberate cover - worth deciding before this surfaces again.

## The Truth

Vanya wasn't cooperative with the OOTS's initiative at the Vault. She was quietly displaced rather than made an example of publicly - no struggle, no scene, no records updated - and is now being held at the [Goblin Settlement](../../../../Quests/Rumours%20&%20Jobs/Solvetra/Rumours/Goblin%20Settlement.md) on the road to Ars Lapis, which answers to the Lorewarden. Her office being untouched and her absence going unaddressed is the OOTS covering its tracks quietly, not staff incompetence. Her office key (Vault Key 1) is still sitting on her desk, unrecovered.

---

## Session 11-12 - Found

The party raided the [Goblin Settlement](../../../../Quests/Rumours%20&%20Jobs/Solvetra/Rumours/Goblin%20Settlement.md) specifically to find her - their intent being to use her to settle the debt with [Lucien Morvault](../../../Villains/Solvetra/Lucien%20Morvault.md), not to resolve the vault keys.

**Session 12** ended with the party reaching the boss room of the ruins beneath the chateau, bloodied and battered, and finding **Vanya in a cage - beaten, bleeding, and crying**. Lightbender Yeesal is still standing; the fight has not happened yet, and she has not spoken.

Her lines are still pending delivery: *"Solvetra Vault... they're taking the money"* / *"It's all the Division"*.

---

## As an Inside Asset

Too broken to be useful at first - she recovers over time.

- **The 4th lock is new.** It was added after she was removed, precisely because she wouldn't cooperate. She managed three locks and signed for three; she doesn't know a fourth exists, and she'll say so with total confidence.
- **Her routine is stale.** She's been gone weeks and doesn't remember the day-to-day reliably - and Vasse's daily rotating countersign went in after her time.
- **She has to keep her head down if she makes it back to Solvetra.** The OOTS erased her with no record trail; a living Vanya is a witness who shouldn't exist.
