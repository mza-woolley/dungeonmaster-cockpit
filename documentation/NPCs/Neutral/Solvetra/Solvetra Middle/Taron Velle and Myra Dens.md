# Innkeeper Taron Velle & Housemaid Myra Dens

**Role:** Innkeeper (Taron) and Housemaid (Myra), The Final Rest
**Where Met:** The Final Rest, Solvetra Middle — Session 5/6

Taron is gruff but reliable, keeps "the really good, dark ale" behind the bar for the right people. Myra is quick and quiet, more aware of who's-meeting-who in the common room than she lets on.

---

## Quest Tie-In

**The Final Rest is where Silas Shadowfoot arranged to meet the party** ("ask the Innkeeper for the really good, dark ale" — the password/signal). Taron is the go-between for that meeting; Myra likely clocks the rendezvous happening.
