# "The Veiled Songbird" & Stagehand Darik Nol

**Role:** Performer (The Veiled Songbird) and Stagehand (Darik Nol), The False Stage
**Where Met:** The False Stage, Solvetra Middle - Session 5

The Veiled Songbird performs masked, voice and identity both hidden - theatrical, deliberately mysterious. Darik runs the practical side: lights, props, keeping the illusion intact.

---

## Quest Tie-In

Session 5: **Kaelen evaporated "the bards" at the False Stage using the Måneklang**, and in doing so heard about the **"Echo Unheard."** The Veiled Songbird and/or the bards present may have been the ones lost here - worth confirming whether the Songbird survived or was among them. The "Echo Unheard" name is a new dangling thread tied directly to this location.
