# Tailendra

**Race/Class:** Human, Town Crier / Newspaper Vendor
**Role:** Solvetra Intermellum's news source — part town-yeller, part paperboy
**Where Met:** Streets of Solvetra Intermellum

Loud, fast-talking, always working the crowd — half the district's gossip and half its actual news comes filtered through Tailendra one way or another. Sells physical newspapers and yells the headlines to anyone in earshot who won't pay.
