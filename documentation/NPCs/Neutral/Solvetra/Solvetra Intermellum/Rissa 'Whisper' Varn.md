# Rissa "Whisper" Varn

**Race:** Halfling
**Role:** Information broker who charges for her "whispers"
**Where Met:** Solvetra Intermellum

> **DM Only - not known to players:** Rissa is the DOL's mole inside the Shadowbringers' orbit - the live replacement for the Session 5 spy who died at The Final Rest (see [Lorewarden Emeritus](../../../Villains/Solvetra/Lorewarden%20Emeritus.md)), reporting to L.E. (likely via an intermediary). She's a separate node from that dead spy - the network was thinned, not fully burned. Her info-broker cover fits cleanly: selling the party heist intel and selling the DOL intel on the Shadowbringers isn't a contradiction for her, just two transactions - and the anti-Detect Thoughts amulet below is tradecraft, not just a broker's paranoia.
>
> **She sandbags** - she does not pass the Vault heist plan to L.E., even after Frah'nk handed her the target, motive, and timeline outright in Session 8. She's read the heist as inevitable regardless of what she does, and her real play is quietly cashing out her own Vault tie-ins ahead of it, not selling a warning that changes nothing for her.
>
> She genuinely does not know the Goblin Settlement is where Vanya Windros is being held - her Session 9 tip was the real limit of what she'd heard, not a controlled leak. Nothing tips the party off before the heist; L.E. dangles a vague post-heist remark instead, and it's on the party to connect it back to her. If exposed, she'll hear it before the party finishes connecting the dots and will already be gone, on the road to Ars Lapis.

Angsty, emo features, dark hair, bitchy "Derry Girl" voice. Holds information about the bank's inside contact:
- DC 1-24: Tells PCs to "eat her boke" - info costs 150GP.
- DC 25+/Nat 20: Reveals she stands to get rich off this; offers 25GP now / 75GP later (one-time deal).
- If PCs try Detect Thoughts, she pulls out an amulet and calls them out - not her first rodeo with mind-readers.

## Session 8

Grakh flagged to Frah'nk that Whisper was looking for him and pointed him her way; Elaris came along. Over the conversation:
- Whisper asked whether Frah'nk wanted blueprints and whether he'd spoken to [Lira Vance](Lira%20Vance.md) (the Archivist) yet.
- She pressed him on the party's inside contact at the bank, since it conflicts with her own existing operations there.
- Frah'nk offered her an in at the Bonds Office in exchange.
- Frah'nk told her outright that he's planning to rob Solvetra Vault. She warned this would badly hurt her and a lot of other people if he goes through with it.
- Frah'nk revealed the debt is owed to **Lucien Morvault** - she confirmed he's in serious trouble.
- She wrapped up by promising to send a note for Frah'nk to visit Lira the next day, and said she'd have extra information for him then.

## Session 9

The "extra information" promised above, delivered secondhand via **Grakh** rather than a direct scene (see [Grakh](../../../Allies/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Grakh.md)): 3 keys are needed for the Vault, and Vanya Windros held one of the manager/master keys. She's missing - Whisper's heard whispers connecting her disappearance to Goblin activity in the city, but isn't sure of the source herself. (DM ground truth, not yet revealed to players: this points to the [Goblin Settlement](../../../../Quests/Rumours%20&%20Jobs/Solvetra/Rumours/Goblin%20Settlement.md), where the OOTS is quietly holding her after she stopped cooperating with the Vault's initiative.)
