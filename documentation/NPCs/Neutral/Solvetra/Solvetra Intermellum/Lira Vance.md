# Lira Vance (Archivist)

**Role:** Sells maps/blueprints of old Solvetra buildings
**Where Met:** Solvetra Intermellum

Has a shelf of maps behind her, revealed via Insight Check (DC 1-10: The Gilded Anvil, DC 10-14: The Bathhouse, DC 14-20: The Solstice Vault, DC 22+/Nat 20: The Lumination Archive). Sells these for 20GP, 40GP, 100GP respectively, and requires a DC 21+/Nat 20 Speech Check to part with the Lumination Archive map (advantage if PCs give a good, fun/relevant reason).
