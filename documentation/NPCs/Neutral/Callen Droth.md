# Callen Droth (Dyemaster)

**Role:** Runs the Dye House; dyes proof vault/bank notes for Solvetra Vault
**Where Met:** The Dye House, Solvetra Intermellum (recommended by Du'geshk)

Found busy dyeing armour and civilian orders amid steaming cauldrons of coloured liquid, plus a station dedicated to dyeing paper notes. A little short with PCs — not from anger, just overloaded with orders, especially the priority Solvetra Vault job.
- Group Insight DC 15+: He does the bank notes for Solvetra Vault, which go straight into the vault — PCs would need real authority to secure any.
- Group Insight DC Nat 20: PCs recall hearing about Internships at Solvetra Vault recently — a possible angle in.
