# Kaeren Arguback

**Race:** Elf
**Role:** Bank Teller (middle desk), Supervisor
**Where Met:** Solvetra Vault, Level 1

Feisty woman who combats players directly, telling them they need a Form 32B for a Working Bank Rights Card and Valid ID. DC 25+/Nat 20 on her own Speech rolls. Will ask for the player's mother's maiden name and closest relative's name for "security," then helps fill out Form 64GB for the Working Bank Rights Card and Valid ID.
