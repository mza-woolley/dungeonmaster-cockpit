# Gruunsh'ck Diamondholder

**Race:** Goblin
**Role:** Bank Teller (rightmost desk)
**Where Met:** Solvetra Vault, Level 1

Nerdy little goblin who "akshually"s the players while walking them through the bureaucratic Form 32B pipeline: Form 1A (chosen class) → Form 1475L (goals/ambitions) → Form 69X (sexual activity in last 45 days) → Form 32B (Bank Teller test — what would you do if someone tried to steal all the gold; acceptable answers range from immediate surrender to hitting the panic button).
