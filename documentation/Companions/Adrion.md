# Adrion

**Status:** Former PC - now NPC. Player left the group IRL after Session 6; Adrion remains in the world as a companion/plot figure rather than being written out.

---

## Overview

Originally one of the party's own, Adrion's history is the source of two of the campaign's live threads: his debt to Lucien Morvault and his unread contract with Zatharial. Both predate his departure as a player character and remain active regardless - the party inherited them by association.

---

## Background

- His home village was burned down; he escaped with his life.
- To buy that escape, he struck a bargain with **Lucien Morvault**: in exchange for getting out alive, Adrion agreed to hand over the next adventuring party he fell in with (the PCs) to Morvault. The 5,000gp demanded in Session 7 is framed as a buyout clause for that original term - see [Lucien Morvault](../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Lucien%20Morvault.md).
- In Solvetra West, **Zatharial** offered Adrion a teleport to rejoin the party in exchange for signing a contract - Adrion signed without reading it. The contract binds Adrion's soul and the souls of the party to Zatharial upon Adrion's death. See [Adrion's Devil Contract](../Story%20Threads/Villians/Adrion's%20Devil%20Contract.md).
- It was this teleport that brought Adrion (and by extension the introduction) to the party at Solvetra West - see [Session 4](../Sessions/Notes/Session%204%20-%20Solvetra%20West%201.md).

---

## Notes

- Played as a full party member through Session 6 (Lumina Lab); confronted over the burned village in Session 5/6.
- Going forward, treat Adrion as an NPC the rest of the party knows well - his debt and contract are the inciting incidents for the Solvetra Vault heist. Zatharial's reveal was originally planned for Session 8 but didn't happen - still pending, as is the heist itself.
- See [Solvetra Vault: Consequence Framework](../In%20Progress/Solvetra%20Vault%3A%20Consequence%20Framework.md) for how both claims resolve.
