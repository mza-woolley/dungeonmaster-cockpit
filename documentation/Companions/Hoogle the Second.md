# Hoogle the Second

**Species:** Worg  
**Owner:** Kaelen  
**Status:** Active companion

---

## Overview

A Worg adopted by Kaelen during the Lumina Lab infiltration (Session 6). Found captive in the Lab's second floor alongside other abused Worgs kept for Lumina Elixir production. Named in honour of Hoogle the First.

---

## Hoogle the First

The original Hoogle - killed by the Goblin Warriors stationed at the Lumina Lab prior to the party's arrival. Kaelen learned of this during the infiltration. The adoption of Hoogle the Second was a direct response.

---

## Notes

- Participated in combat during Session 6 (Initiative D10, rolled 11)
- The Worgs at the Lab were systematically abused; Hoogle the Second is a survivor of that operation
- The goblin responsible for Hoogle the First's death is among those the party killed
