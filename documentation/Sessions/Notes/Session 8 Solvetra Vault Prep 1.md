# Session 8 — Solvetra Vault Prep 1

---

## Introduction

The vault job is still a shape in the fog — no break-in, no plan locked, just threads to pull. Plumbodian's internship keeps paying off: paperwork, an "X" that Gruunsh'ck let slide, and an errand to the Dye House that puts real numbers in front of you for the first time. Twenty-five thousand gold, more than once, all addressed to the same name — Lucien Morvault. The man Adrion owes. The man you owe now.

The rest of the party's day looks smaller by comparison — herbs, a Banshee that shouldn't have been there, a "danger fee" argued out of a shopkeeper who doesn't yet know he's been robbed. But it's Frah'nk's evening that raises the stakes: Whisper knows about the vault plan now, knows about Morvault, and isn't happy about either. She's sending you to Lira Vance tomorrow, with more to say once you get there.

## Session Notes

*What actually happened. Filled in during/after the session.*

- Kaelen and co. visited the Wayfarer's Cache and accepted a quest to pick up herbs — confirmed to be chamomile tea, 200gp reward.
- Cut to Plumbodian at the bank, working through a stack of intern paperwork (Sleight of Hand) — each paper confirms a citizen's bond/tender being zeroed out to $0.
- The PCs (Elaris, Frah'nk, Fenrik, Wizzleforth) headed north to the forest clearing to collect flowers.
- A Banshee was heard and investigated — Frah'nk sensed it was Undead. Wizzleforth's Turn Undead failed to affect it (Banshee succeeded its save).
- Fenrik covered the Banshee's head with a sack; Frah'nk took the first shot, starting the fight. PCs defeated the Banshee, taking heavy damage.
- Back at the bank, Gruunsh'ck reviewed Plumbodian's paperwork and accepted an "X" signature (Deception 16). Sent Plumbodian to the Dye House in Solvetra Intermellum to see Callen Droth. Dropped lore that Solvetra Vault isn't currently letting people withdraw their money.
- Kaelen, back at the Wayfarer's Cache, cast Suggestion on Ronan twice (first attempt resisted) to make him hand over his gold and a note. Also stole a gold ring, 2 silver rings, 35gp, 2 Potions of Healing, and 1 Greater Healing Potion. Ronan ran out into public naked.
- The rest of the party returned to the Wayfarer's Cache with the sack, the chamomile, and the Banshee's head. Kaelen rejoined them disguised.
- At the Dye House, Callen asked Plumbodian to take the bank notes back to Solvetra Vault, but skimmed a cut of the cash first. On the way back, Plumbodian ran into the PC party — together they went through the bonds and confirmed multiple 25,000gp bonds addressed to Lucien Morvault.
- Back at the Vault, Gruunsh'ck confirmed no one is addressing Vanya's continued absence — bonds just keep getting processed with no questions asked. Plumbodian finished his paperwork.
- Party collected their reward for Ronan's herbs; argued a "danger fee" over the Banshee encounter (Ronan meanwhile realizing he'd been robbed by Kaelen) and were allowed to take stock from the shop as compensation.
- Fenrik and Wizzleforth checked the notice board; Frah'nk, Elaris, and Kaelen headed back to the Smuggler's Den (Elaris introduced herself to Grakh); Plumbodian kept working.
- Grakh told Frah'nk that Whisper was looking for him. Frah'nk and Elaris met with Whisper — she asked about blueprints and whether he'd spoken to Lira Vance, pressed him about his inside contact conflicting with her own operations, and Frah'nk offered her an in at the Bonds Office. Frah'nk told her he's planning to rob Solvetra Vault; she warned it would badly hurt her and a lot of other people.
- Frah'nk revealed the debt is owed to Lucien Morvault — Whisper confirmed he's in serious trouble. She said she'd send a note for him to visit Lira Vance the next day and would have extra information for him then.
- Everyone but Fenrik turned in for the night. Fenrik went to the Chamber of Unspoken Prayer and found Halden holding the door shut against skeletons trying to break in — setup for next session's fight.
