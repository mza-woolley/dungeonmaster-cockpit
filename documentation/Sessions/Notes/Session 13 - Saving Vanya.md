## Session Notes

*What actually happened. Filled in during/after the session.*

*Sessions 13 and 14 were improvised without notes. This covers both, reconstructed afterwards from the DM's summary; the exact split between the two sessions wasn't recorded.*

- Party **beat Lightbender Yeesal** and **saved Vanya**. Yeesal's patron (the Lorewarden) didn't come up.
- Took Vanya out and found a **locked safe** in the fort with information on what was going on: Lumina Elixir barrels with **"return" labels from Ars Lapis**. The barrels go back and forth, so production never truly halted. Gold from the safe let the party settle with Morvault. The barrels were left behind.
- On the way out, **Lucien Morvault** appeared and demanded payment. The party **paid the balance in full**. He opened with a "roll call" to intimidate them and called Plumbodian **"the 3rd"** - confusion all round. (DM: Plumbodian has had multiple lives; Morvault knows, Plumbodian doesn't.)
- **Vanya revealed it was Headguard Valerius** who put her in the fort.
- Party rested at a small inn on the way back so Vanya could recover. Coined the name **"The LeChoixian Six"**.
- Heard about **Dorian Vasse** from NPCs and commoners, having missed the Headguard announcement - "a bit of a wet noodle".
- Found a **ledger** of sales in the fort. Didn't mean much to the party.
- **Frah'nk sold the ledger to Whisper** for **1,500gp now, 4,500gp when she "cashes out"**. She says she's escaping Solvetra and taking the road, and will adventure alongside the party to Ars Lapis. (DM: she bought it to keep it from the party and return it to the Lorewarden.)
- **Vanya is hidden upstairs at the Smuggler's Den.** The plan is to help her "escape" to Lunarthorn.
- Next day: a **shopping episode** for the journey to Ars Lapis. At the **Arcane Resonator**, a mystery box (gold in, item out) gave nothing notable except **Frah'nk's axe** - explicitly an **agent of Bhaal**. No personality and not Bhaal himself, but it endeavours to slaughter endlessly. Frah'nk tested it on some bees. Named by the group (name not recorded).
- **Vault heist shelved.**
- Party got ready to leave the next day.
