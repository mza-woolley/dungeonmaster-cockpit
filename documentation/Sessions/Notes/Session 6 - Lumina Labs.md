***INTRODUCTION:***

Valerius's confrontation ended not in blood, but confession-he sent the thugs, yet let the party walk free. In two nights, they'll meet Silas at the Final Rest.

Adrion wrestled with accusations over his burned village, while Plumbodian bent to the Lorewarden's will. Deep in his studies, Wizzleforth began to see the Lumination for what it is.

Kaelen reforged the smith's flame and armed his allies, only to turn that power on the False Stage, erasing its bards in a burst of sound and unveiling a name whispered in dread: the Echo Unheard.

Their path darkened; a ghoul battle left corpses and questions. And as the city held its breath, Silas returned with a warning: Lumina Labs is not what it seems… and that goblins walk beneath the light.

**LUMINA LABS:**

**Level 1:**
(Passive) Documents on bench, detailing the process in which how to make the "Lumina Elixir".
(DC12) Blueprints of a filtration system labeled "Stage III: Salivary Infusion Chamber."
(DC17) A logbook showing the production quota linked to a mysterious "Overseer."
(DC20) Distribution Map, plan to distribute throughout Ars Lapis. Signed, L.E.
(Nat20, or DC25) To ensure safe passage, ensure to greet goblins with "Hail the Order, the Order of the Sun".

Fight: 3 × Goblin Warrior, and a surprise 1 × Goblin Boss around the corner.

**Level 2:**
(Passive) 3 dead, 1 sleeping, abused Worgs in cages, 1 × Goblin (Warrior) watching over them. Many of the distilleries that you had seen before. For each Worg, two balls and chain and maces on the ground \- with a mix of dirty pawprints and lots of seemingly lost blood.
Goblin watching the cages is not paying attention to the entrance.
(DC13) Players can see a page on the ground, near the goblin as follows "... you have to beat the Worg so it salivates, or else what's the point?"
(DC14) PCs can identify that it may possible to sneak on by.
(DC17) Diary next to bed can be seen. PC can read as follows "...and that was a brilliant hunt, shame the other lads didn't make it out".
(Nat20, or DC22) "...the hefty sum paid to kill that dog was free money. I wonder why the boss requested that?"

Fight: 1 × Goblin Warrior, 2 × Sleeping Goblin Warriors.

**Level 3:**
(Passive) PCs walk downstairs, to see a sleeping Goblin in a chair, looking to be some sort of checkpoint past the pathway he seems to be watching. There are some pamphlets \+ a book open on his desk.
(DC14) Logbook has a list of visitors. On the list: "Our Lord", "Boss Dragg", "H.V".
(DC17) Pamphlets under the log book, as follows "We have a special visitor today, do NOT, fuck this up!"
PCs can convince the doorman (DC12) to let players through; they should be there.
Once they approach the Goblin Boss, he'll ask for a pass signed by The Boss (they won't have one, persuasion DC20)

Fight: 1 × Goblin Boss and 1 × Goblin Warrior, the turn after.

PCs will notice a river that's flowing through toward a rocky area, in which the Goblin Boss seems to have been doorman'ing for. There's a rocky tunnel through to another side of the door. They'll also notice some different alchemist equipment that is labelled: "Worg Tears", "Worg Blood", "Worg Saliva", "Boiled and Filtered River Water", "Secret Herb Mix".

PCs will notice a table nearby with a few Potion(s) of Healing on them (×4).
There's also ×2 Potion of Spell Restore (gives back 1 × Spell Slot).
There's also ×1 Sharing Potion of Long Rest (4 × Uses)

**Cave 1:**
(Passive) PCs walk in and notice massive potion vials and vats similar to what they saw earlier through the floors, but at this stage they are huge. It's a large, open and rocky area with stone pillars coming from the ceiling.
(Passive) There's a lone goblin at the bridge over the water, looks to be analysing and mixing ingredients out of the different large vats into labelled bottles; "Lumina Elixir".

PCs will encounter and fight Boss "Dragg". Dragg's DC for Persuasion/Deception/etc is DC20.
Whatever the result of this, afterwards, Valerius will soon appear.
He will briefly address the party before attacking them.

Players can (DC12 Investigation) a note on the body.
"Our special guests should be arriving to stop our operation. I've organised your replacement already. Do not fail me or I will smother your light. \- The Order".

Silas will comment on "The Order" and can explain this to players very briefly.
