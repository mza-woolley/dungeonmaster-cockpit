# Ars Lapis Arc - Playbook (S15-S20)

---

## How to use this

Each session has a **locked start** and a **locked end**. Those are the rails. Everything in **During** is flexible: drop it, reorder it, or swap it out as earlier sessions develop. **Rails** lists the ways to steer the party back toward the end scene when they wander.

**Arc premise:** Whisper's reports and the Lumina Lab finally being traced back to them have told the Lorewarden it was the party all along. He wants them gone quietly, through hired killers and a legal frame, before anyone above him looks closely at his Elixir business. In Ars Lapis the party uncovers what the Elixir is really for: **the Gem Families dose their miners in Echo Veins to keep them working.** The city's wealth runs on burnt-out labour.

**Lead tone:** conspiracy to unravel. Flavour: the faceless machine (contracts, law), the personal villain (the Lorewarden, felt before he's seen), and power to resist (the miners).

**Lorewarden in Ars Lapis:** the City Framework says he has *no* standing there, because he's only big in a small town. Use that. He's out of his depth and has to **buy** his killers and his frame from bigger players. By S19 those players are ready to cut him loose, which is why his deal offer might be sincere. He's desperate.

## Session 15 - The Road Out

**START (locked):** Morning at the Smuggler's Den. The barkeep hands over Whisper's sealed letter: *"Business to finish. Meet me at Ars Lapis, I'll have your coin. Don't wait up. - W."* The wax seal carries a faint sunburst. It means nothing now and will hit hard later.

**During:**
*Order of events:*

1. **Leave with Vanya.** She comes with them, smuggled out (cart, disguise, crate).

2. **The broken cart.** OOTS guards are trying to fix a broken Elixir cart with "RETURN" stamped barrels. The guards are **not** on the Elixir. They're sober, competent, and **know exactly what they're transporting**. They're hostile from the start: *"Move along. Nothing to see here."*
   - **Hidden:** goblins from the fort are in the back of the cart, and the PCs can't see them at first. Reveal them through a noise, a smell, the tarp shifting, or a Perception check if anyone lingers.
   - This connects to the fort: the goblins there greeted the party with *"Hail the Order, the Order of the Sun"*, and here the OOTS is moving the survivors out.
   - **Vanya:** if the guards spot her, that's a problem, because officially she doesn't exist.

3. **NSPF ambush on Fenrik.** They knew the route and the timing (Whisper). The leader's orders say "the dragon-touched one, dead." The written order is signed **"ABE"** (Aurevon Beitomy Erstua, NSPF leader) and also carries a **second seal: L.E.'s**. Goons kill themselves before talking under interrogation. If one breaks before he can, he lets slip "friends in the Division".

4. **The long road.** Drop in one or two of these. Roll a d8 or pick:

   | d8 | Event |
   | :--- | :--- |
   | 1 | **The toll bridge.** An old halfling charges a 5gp toll on "his" bridge, which is a log over a creek. He has a hand-painted sign, a ledger, and a very official hat. |
   | 2 | **Genuine Ars Lapis gems.** A fast-talking pedlar sells "authentic Lumina Gems" that are painted glass. A taste of hustle city before they arrive. |
   | 3 | **The goat.** A runaway goat decides one PC is its mother and will not leave. Its owner turns up later, furious and convinced they stole it. |
   | 4 | **Bard-off.** Two rival bards racing to an audition in Ars Lapis demand the party judge a song-off. The loser blames the judges, loudly, forever. |
   | 5 | **Gnome in the mud.** A gnome inventor's wagon is stuck. He insists on using his own elaborate pulley contraption, which fails spectacularly in stages. |
   | 6 | **The milestone.** A signpost reading "Ars Lapis - 12 miles" is a mimic. The real sign is behind it. Both are wrong about the distance. |
   | 7 | **Shelter from the storm.** A sudden downpour. The only cover is a hollow tree already occupied by a grumpy badger family who do not want to share. |
   | 8 | **The thirsty axe.** Frah'nk's axe wants blood. It pulls toward every rabbit, deer and passing traveller. A WIS save at camp, or it "wanders" in the night. |

**Lunarthorn (player choice: before or after Ars Lapis).** The road forks toward the pinewoods. If they go now, it happens here, between the road events and arriving at Ars Lapis. If they go later, Vanya stays with the party to the walls and on into S16, where she's a liability in a city of contracts and papers.
   - The pinewoods go quiet and moonlight seems to follow them. The enclave takes Vanya in without being asked, almost as if they expected her.
   - An elder notices Måneklang (Kaelen) or Elaris's necklace. Keep it to a light seed.
   - **Pacing:** if they go now, keep it to one scene so they still reach the walls by the end of the session.

5. **Arrive at dusk, camp outside the walls.** The gates close at sundown and the contract office opens at dawn, so they can't get in tonight. The city glows above them, lit with Lumina Gems.

**END (locked): Zatharial at the fire.** He arrives with wine and two chairs nobody brought. He's warm, funny and genuinely friendly. He congratulates them on paying Lucien, then tears up Adrion's contract as a goodwill gesture: *"Souls in a ledger collect dust. I'd rather you were moving."* Then: *"Ask me anything. Everything has a price, but I'm very reasonable."*

**His rule:** one question, one price. He names the price **before** he answers, and they can always say no. He never lies, but he loves a technicality.

| Price | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Small** | A trivial memory (the taste of a favourite food, a childhood pet's name), a lock of hair, a song sung for him, or a true answer to *his* question |
| **Medium** | A secret said aloud in front of the whole party, or "a small favour, to be named later" |
| **Large** (rare) | A binding favour, or a year of life. Only for the big questions. |

**Likely questions and his answers:**
- **Why tear up the contract?** *Free.* "People in debt make poor decisions. I need you making good ones."
- **What's inside Ars Lapis?** *Small.* "Everyone in there is selling something. Some of them are selling themselves and haven't noticed."
- **The axe?** *Small.* "A splinter of a very hungry god. It isn't yours, friend. You're its."
- **Plumbodian, "the 3rd"?** *Medium.* "You've worn that name before. More than once. Ask whoever sank your ships."
- **Whisper?** *Medium.* "Your friend with the amulet? Lovely girl. Not your friend."
- **Who is ABE?** *Medium.* "Read the name again. Slowly." (He knows. He's enjoying it.)
- **Who is the Light Vassal?** *Large.* "You've met. You were grateful at the time." (That's Sibling Thorne. Decide on the night how far he goes.)
- **Can you get us inside?** *Refused.* "Doors are more fun when you open them yourselves." This keeps them outside the walls.
- **Won't answer:** his own nature, who he answers to, or Virelle by name. He smiles and changes the subject.

**Optional:** the axe reacts to him (a devil and an agent of Bhaal don't get along), and he calls Plumbodian *"the 5th now, is it?"*

**Exit:** *"When you're ready to choose, say my name."* The chairs are gone in the morning. The wine isn't.

**Rails:** there's only one road, Vanya needs to get out of Solvetra, Whisper "owes them 4,500gp" at the other end, and the gates are shut for the night. **They do not enter Ars Lapis this session.**

## Session 16 - The Gates of Stone

**START (locked):** The gates of Ars Lapis. Outsiders need a **sanctioned contract** to enter. The party has none, so the only quick way in is to sign a **temporary labour contract** with a Gem Family. This is the railroad: it puts them in the mines.

**During:**
- First look at the city: New York hustle with LA desperation. Inventors showing off magic and potions, investors circling. Some of the most brilliant are clearly on the Elixir.
- **Bankrupt Promenade:** the end-stage users who burned out. The "spark in a bottle" made visible.
- **If Vanya is still with them:** she has no papers and officially doesn't exist, so the gate check is a problem. Lunarthorn becomes a detour back out of the city, or she hides at the camp.
- **Whisper no-show:** they go to the meeting place and nobody's there. A clerk says no one by that name has passed through the gates.
- The jobs board and street rumours are a gold-first break (Ars Lapis Jobs/Rumours are empty, so fill them as needed).
- **Echo Veins orientation:** a foreman shows them the shift system. They meet one miner NPC who becomes **the face of the miners** (name them on the night).

**END (locked):** The first night shift. The party watches the **shift ration** being handed out, and the "RETURN" barrels from S15 are being refilled at the mine's mouth. A miner next to them drinks, works like a machine, then stops mid-swing with a blank stare.

**Rails:** the labour contract binds them to shifts and the gate guards hold their sanctioned papers. Walking away means being an illegal in the city.

## Session 17 - Below the Stone

**START (locked):** Waking up in the miners' barracks to the ration line. Now they know what's in the cup.

**During:**
- **Unravel:** shift records, dosing schedules, a foreman's book. The supply chain runs Lumina Lab → Ars Lapis, and the paperwork echoes Whisper's ledger.
- **Kaelen and Echo Veins:** deep in the mine, Måneklang picks up the same hum as "The Echo Unheard", older and coming from below. One sensory beat, no answers.
- **Introduce the DOL's face in Ars Lapis** (still to be named): tech-bro energy, owns the Stock Exchange, manipulates the Gem Families. He's polite and curious about the new labourers, and he clearly already knows someone is looking for them.
- **Silas reaches out** (letter, messenger, or in person). There are no Shadowbringers in Ars Lapis, so he wants the party to *be* his presence here. His pitch is the Stock Exchange (the DOL skims 3.5%). He sees the party as his hands.
- The party's miner NPC opens up about friends who "went quiet" and were shipped out.

**END (locked):** The party's miner NPC is found dead at the bottom of a shaft and written up as an **accident**. On the body is something that points straight at the party: their contract number, or a weapon or item that belongs to them. The frame has started.

**Rails:** the mine is a closed system, so evidence is down there and so is the danger.

## Session 18 - The Frame

**START (locked):** City guards at the barracks door at dawn, with a warrant. Ars Lapis law is rigid and contract-based, so the party is now criminals in the system.

**During:**
- **Running and hiding.** The miners decide whether to hide them. This is the first real "power to resist" beat: the miners choose the party over the foremen.
- **Hired killers in the city:** a second, better-funded NSPF or contract-killer team. They're paid in Lumina Gems, not gold (a clue that someone in Ars Lapis is paying, not the Lorewarden).
- **Clear their name, or prove the frame:** who planted the evidence? The trail leads to a Gem Family enforcer, then to a payment, then to **L.E.'s money.**
- **The DOL face** quietly lets them know he's aware of all this. He finds it entertaining and is in no hurry to protect the Lorewarden.

**END (locked):** **The Lorewarden Emeritus in person.** They last saw him in his office in S9, where he was comfortable and in control. Here he's out of place, sweating in a city that doesn't know his name. He makes an offer: *"I can make the warrant disappear. I need you to do something for me."* Leave it on the table. Don't let them answer yet.

**Rails:** the warrant means they can't leave the city, and the offer is their only obvious way out.

## Session 19 - Three Doors

**START (locked):** Safe-house. The Lorewarden's offer has a **deadline** (dawn, a shift change, a public execution date). Then a second visitor arrives: Silas, or a message from him.

**During - the three pitches:**
- **The Lorewarden:** *"Help me bring down the man above me here before he throws me to the wolves."* Is it sincere? Keep it ambiguous. He *is* desperate, and he *is* the one who tried to kill them.
- **Silas / the Shadowbringers:** crash the Stock Exchange and cripple the DOL's cash flow. It's a real cause, but it hurts the city's ordinary people too.
- **Zatharial:** says his name and he's there. He offers power, a way out and a clean slate, in return for one day facing the Light Vassal. He's charming, he doesn't push, and the price is real.
- **The miners' option:** the party's NPC's friends want a **strike**, the city's true pressure point. They need the proof the party holds.
- Prep for whatever they choose: gather proof of the dosing, recruit miners, case the Exchange.

**END (locked):** The choice is made, and **someone pays for it.** Whichever side they *didn't* pick acts against them, or the side they picked shows its true cost. For example: the Lorewarden sells them out anyway, Silas leaks their names, or Zatharial's price comes due sooner than he said.

**Rails:** the deadline forces a decision inside this session.

## Session 20 - Breaking the Stone

**START (locked):** The plan goes live at a set-piece moment: shift change, a Gem Family showcase, or the Stock Exchange's opening bell.

**During:**
- The miners learn what's in the ration, and the strike begins. Echo Veins goes silent and the city's economy stalls in real time.
- The Gem Families' private enforcers try to break the strike by force, and the party stands with the miners.
- **The Lorewarden's fate:** cut loose by the DOL face, by the party, or both. The Framework says "what happens to him may be relevant", and this is where it lands.
- The Stock Exchange (if Silas's route was taken): chaos for investors and a hit to the DOL's cash flow.

**END (locked):** The city's grip is broken, or at least cracked. Then the DOL face looks *up*, toward someone higher. Somewhere, **Virelle** notices for the first time. Elaris feels it as warmth on her neck, or her necklace burning cold. That's the hook into the next arc.

**Rails:** the climax happens whether they're ready or not.
