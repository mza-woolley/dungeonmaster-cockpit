# Session 12 - Goblin Camp Depths

Basic Prep:

- Inside of Goblin Camp depths. Notes for what happens inside included in PNG of "goblinCampUnderNotes.png".

- Vanya is kept in Room 8 (boss room). She will be beaten and battered, on the brink of conciousness. She will be unable to really speak, and just say things to the effect of 

"Solvetra Vault... they're taking the money"..."It's all the Division"....

- Riddle for puzzle, on a stone slab as PC's walk in (1,3,4,7,11,18):

The famous twins begin as one,
but here the elder's shadow comes undone.
I start alone, then leap ahead to three,
and every step thereafter follows me:
look back two paces, add what you have trod,
and that sum marks the stone that will not nod.
Six stones to cross, from threshold to the gate.
Step wrong, and the arrow will seal your fate.

- Consequences for puzzle:

Correct: Door simply opens to allow passage.

Incorrect: For reach wrong attempt, arrows blast out at the party, 1d8 damage. They can block these, etc if they clue on. Arrows come from pillars in the roof.
