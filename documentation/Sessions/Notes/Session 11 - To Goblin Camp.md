# Session 11 - To Goblin Camp

---

## Introduction

*A narrative recap of last session's events and a hook into this one. Written in second-person present tense - describe what the players remember and where they left off, ending on a cliffhanger or inciting moment.*

The vault hasn't moved. Four sessions of prep and it's still just sitting there in Intermellum, three locks deep, and not one key in anyone's pocket. What you do have is a name and a direction: Vanya Windros, bank manager, missing since before any of you started counting - and Whisper's tip, passed through Grakh, that the whispers point at goblins.

Half a day's trek east, off the Ars Lapis road. One day there and back, if it's clean.

It won't be clean.

## Session Prep / Direction

*Key beats, NPC dialogue notes, planned direction for the session. How it's supposed to go.*

Per [Goblin Settlement](../../Quests/Rumours%20&%20Jobs/Solvetra/Rumours/Goblin%20Settlement.md) - low-stakes, hack-and-slash by design. Kaelen's revenge arc; also just a power trip.

**1. On the road (~30 min)**
Travel east off the Ars Lapis road. Hoogle the Second sniffs out the scent/lead toward the camp - if Hoogle's unavailable, Plumbodian hears something goblin-ish instead.

**2. The road ambush (~45 min)**
3-man goblin ambush jumps the party on the road. In the dying moments of the fight, one goblin shouts to the other: **"You need to warn the camp!"** - whether that goblin escapes is the hinge for how braced the camp is.

**3. The camp exterior (~90 min)**
~18 goblins total, occupying an old lunar fortress. Word of the Lumina Lab's destruction has likely reached them, so they're jumpy and bracing - not ambushed, just on edge.

**4. The elixir carts - consequential reveal**
3 carts of Lumina Elixir, loaded and labelled for shipment to Ars Lapis - one per Gem Family. The reveal: the party did **not** actually stop production. It's far larger than they could have conceived.

**5. Close on the descent**
End on the way down into the depths - sets up Session 12.

## Session Notes

*What actually happened. Filled in during/after the session.*

- Party left Solvetra heading for the Goblin Settlement. **Motivation note:** they picked this over the heist specifically hoping to find the bank manager and use her to settle the debt with [Lucien Morvault](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Lucien%20Morvault.md) - *not* the Hoogle revenge arc. (DM note: Kaelen's revenge framing never became the driver; the camp got pulled on as debt leverage instead. Worth reframing the quest doc around that.)
- On the way out of town the party ran into the **halflings Fenrik had antagonised** at the Solvetra Windmill (the cart he kicked over in Session 9). It turned into a fight - the party killed a few and left the rest incapacitated. (DM note: a daylight killing in/around Middle Solvetra, and Kaelen is *already* on the guards' books for the Wayfarer's Cache robbery. Completely unaddressed so far - this is a live consequence, not a throwaway.)
- Stopped at an inn just out of town to hear out rumours. The **innkeep** reported adventurers coming back her way with accounts of Goblins.
- An **Order of the Sun patrol unit** stopped the party and flatly contradicted the innkeep's account. They spot-searched the party and found nothing of note. (DM note: of course they contradicted it - they're in on it; the camp answers to the Lorewarden. This is the first time the OOTS has actively run interference in front of the party, and the party has no reason yet to read it as a lie.)
- Party pushed on and rested in a forest clearing, taking watch shifts.
- Kaelen and Plumbodian broke off to scout what looked like an older **chateau/castle** and got caught in a couple of **bear traps** - leaving **Elaris alone on watch**.
- **Morvault appeared.** He stepped out of a portal with no warning and threatened Elaris intensely - the party dies if the money isn't produced. (DM note: run as intimidation/parley, never a fight, per the bargain's leash. He went to Elaris alone and isolated on purpose.)
- Party reconvened and worked out they now have roughly **18 hours** to settle with Morvault. Hard clock, now running.
- Moved on the chateau in daylight. They observed **barrels being carted in and out** of the ruins, and Kaelen clocked **goblins travelling with the carriages**, in stealth.
- **The elixir reveal landed.** The party saw the **barrels set for Ars Lapis along with the shipping manifest(s)** - confirming the Lumina Elixir operation is alive, organised, and shipping at scale to the Gem Families. (DM note: this is the gut-punch - they did **not** actually stop production at the Lab. It's far larger than they could have conceived, and they now know it.)
- Party blew down the big metal-reinforced wooden **front door** and caught **every goblin in the camp** in the surprise round. They came out on top - rolling straight into Session 12.
