Last time, Wizzleforth drank from the Fountain while Plumbodian *pissed into it*, causing the water to steam away. Adrion signed a devil's contract with *Zatharial* without reading it — a deal that may haunt the whole party.

Their search for answers led them to the *Lightstation*, where a bald, mustached man — **Headguard Valerius du Noirveil** — calmly stepped forward and said,
 "You've caused quite the stir. Let's discuss how you intend to fix it."

*And that's where we begin…*

**Session Notes, to be organised later:**

The party has left off confronting a bald, mustached man named: "Valerius du Noirveil".

Valerius has a strong, commanding, High English voice.

- Talks about condemning the current actions of the party, including the strange occurrences of which happened at Solvetra, being the last people seen regarding the murder of the store clerk \- as well as creating a messy job with the hired thugs sent after them.
- Apologies for sending the thugs after them \- admits it was him. Admits that this party is stronger than most.
- Advises that they stop this path they've set upon, as they don't know what kind of fight they are getting themselves into. If there is player resistance, no contest; hand them a pass through the city and say it won't have to be him that deals with them later, so he's unconcerned what you do from here; your presence has been noted and escalated. Notes that they still cannot enter Solvetra Intermellum even with this pass.

Direction:

- PCs meet with Valerius and are effectively sent on their way, barring any further conflict there.
- PCs are free to explore both Solvetra East and Solvetra Middle if they want now, they just can't visit Intermellum as there's a hard lockdown on that area indefinitely.
- PCs meet Silas Shadowfoot, who will serve as the "main quest" giver on the side of the Shadowbringers, a rebellion like order that is trying to "dissolve" the light.
- Silas is a short, brown-haired halfing, wearing what looks to be grey/black coloured scout clothing; travelling light and nimble. Has a bow on his back and quiver at the hip.
- Silas asks players to meet him at The Final Rest "tomorrow night", ask the Innkeeper for the "really good, dark ale".
- He expresses, for now, it's critically important that PCs improve their reputation around town as they'll need to be in good standing if they have any chance of defeating the Order. He mentions that there'll always be people who can use help around town.
- Silas lore dumps regarding the Order of the Sun and who one or two of them are, but he does not know what purpose they serve. Silas will explain that spies of the Shadowbringer saw what happened at Solvetra Meadows and reported back; PCs could be the force needed to turn the battle against the Order.
- Silas wants players to investigate the Lumina Lab. Strange occurrences and transactions have been happening out of that lab. Furthermore, a goblin was spotted there, which is extremely suspicious.
