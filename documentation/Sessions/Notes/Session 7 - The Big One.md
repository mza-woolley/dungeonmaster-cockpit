# Session 7 - The Big One

---

## Introduction

Under the veil of night, the party joined **Silas** to infiltrate the **Lumina Lab**, where whispers spoke of strange experiments behind its shining elixir. Slipping through a hidden trapdoor, they discovered the horrifying truth - the **Lumina Elixir** was brewed through the torment of captive Worgs.

Fighting through Goblin guards, they uncovered a logbook hinting at a mysterious "Lord," and Kaelen claimed a new companion - **Hoogle the Second**, avenging the first. Blood, secrets, and fury filled the halls as **Boss Dragg** and **Valerius du Noirveil** fell before them.

Among the wreckage, a letter read:

"Do not fail. I've already organised your replacement. If you fail - I will smother your light."

Now, as the heroes march toward Solvetra **Intermellum**, the shadow of the **Lumination** looms ever closer…

Will they expose the Order's secret flame, or be consumed by it?

## Session Prep / Direction

### The Morning After
PCs wake in the Smuggler's Den (the Shadowbringers' "Inn" - Silas has vouched for them, so no one bothers them). Everyone except Kaelen, who's with Juno Pell collecting gear, has some changes:

- **Kaelen** - Receives 2 × Shortswords + Scale Mail. Learns about Trance (elves don't need to sleep). Once per short rest, the first time he gives Bardic Inspiration in a battle, Måneklang's Hum triggers: the creature nearest the inspired ally must make a DC8 WIS save or their head explodes with the resonance.
- **Fenrik** - New "Super Series Seriously Serious Flaming Sirius Fist of Series of Seriousness": 3d4+4 dmg, having mastered fighting with multiple fists at once.
- **Plumbodian** - Quarterstaff now channels the Orb's cosmic/divine energy: +8 to hit/DC (up from +3), plus a once-per-battle reroll on any missed attack or failed/missed spell.
- **Wizzleforth** - WIS 17→18, INT 8→10, plus extra proficiency in Religion after a night reading up on the Division of Lumination.

### The Letter
Downstairs, Silas is waiting with a letter addressed to Adrion:

> "The time has come - I require the payment from you. 5,000 gold pieces as we agreed upon. I'll meet you in Solvetra Intermellum in 5 night's time. If I don't see you, I will teleport to you and Mass Polymorph you and any friends of yours into pathetic little bugs I can squash beneath my boot. Your choice, Adrion. - Lucien Morvault"

Silas presses the party on what Adrion's gotten them into - there's no way the PCs have 5,000gp lying around, and only one realistic way to get it.

### Silas's Pitch
Silas admits the timing isn't entirely bad. He and Du'geshk have been sitting on plans to crack the **Solvetra Vault** for years - the Shadowbringers always knew the Lumination's coffers were sitting under their feet, but never had the people to pull it off. Every past attempt was turned away, captured, or worse.

Watching the party walk out of Lumina Lab, Silas reckons this is the first crew he's looked at and thought "these are the ones." He's not pretending it's pure charity - Adrion's debt gives everyone a reason to move *now* - but he's genuinely thrilled the excuse has finally shown up: *"I've wanted this for a long, long time. You being short on Lucien's money? That's not a problem. That's an opportunity."*

### Introducing Frah'nk
During the heist briefing, Du'geshk mentions they're short-handed - good crew, too many moving parts. She nods toward a large orc eating breakfast alone at a corner table: *"He showed up this morning. I know him. He's solid."* That's Frah'nk Frapper - sent by Silvanus, there to assess the party as much as help them. He won't introduce himself beyond two sentences; let the players come to him. See [Frah'nk Frapper](../../Players/Characters/Frah'nk%20Frapper.md).

### Meeting Du'geshk & the Setback
Silas introduces **Du'geshk Maharjuuj** - tall half-orc/half-dragonborn fighter, greatsword + heavy armour, Skyrim-Orc-female voice. She's been running the heist op for the Shadowbringers while Silas handles Division business, and hands the PCs a checklist to plan the job (she'll push them to split up and hit leads simultaneously).

**Corwin Duskwatch** bursts in: progress on the vault codes has stalled because bank manager **Vanya Windros** has vanished - no ransacking, no signs of struggle, she just didn't come back. Without her, getting into the vault (3 locks, must open simultaneously, blast-resistant) means finding all the required keys, and no one's ever seen them all in one place.

> *Note: only 3 locks are known about at this stage - the existence of a 4th is a deliberate surprise for the players (see [Solvetra Vault - Vault Door & Keys](../../../Locations/Dungeons%2C%20Ruins%20%26%20Others/Solvetra%20Vault.md)). Keep Corwin's "3 locks" framing intact when running this scene.*

Silas thanks Corwin and sends him off for a beer. He points the party to **Threadbare Provisions** for heist gear (namedrop him or they won't sell anything), says he'll be upstairs in Ars Lapis if needed, and mentions they're welcome to meet his dad **Silvanus** if they head up there.

PCs are now free to set out and begin investigating the heist. For the leads themselves and the vault layout, see [The Big One - Active Quests](../../../Quests/Active/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Active%20Quests.md) and [Solvetra Vault](../../../Locations/Dungeons%2C%20Ruins%20%26%20Others/Solvetra%20Vault.md). None of the leads are prerequisites for the others, and nothing here forces the vault itself to be hit this session.
