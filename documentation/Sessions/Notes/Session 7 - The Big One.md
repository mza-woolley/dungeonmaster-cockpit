# Session 7 \- The Big One

### Introduction:

Under the veil of night, the party joined **Silas** to infiltrate the **Lumina Lab**, where whispers spoke of strange experiments behind its shining elixir. Slipping through a hidden trapdoor, they discovered the horrifying truth — the **Lumina Elixir** was brewed through the torment of captive Worgs.

Fighting through Goblin guards, they uncovered a logbook hinting at a mysterious "Lord," and Kaelen claimed a new companion — **Hoogle the Second**, avenging the first. Blood, secrets, and fury filled the halls as **Boss Dragg** and **Valerius du Noirveil** fell before them.

Among the wreckage, a letter read:

"Do not fail. I've already organised your replacement. If you fail — I will smother your light."

Now, as the heroes march toward Solvetra **Intermellum**, the shadow of the **Lumination** looms ever closer…

Will they expose the Order's secret flame, or be consumed by it?

# Solvetra Vault L1

Solvetra Vault L1 Notes:

- Players will walk in to see an empty bank. Complete a brief overview of what players can see just based off the map.
- Investigation/Insight:
  - DC Nat 1: This place looks prime for a steal RIGHT now. The guards are sparse, and aren't even paying attention.
  - DC 2-13: Looking around the bank it obviously seems pretty empty. There aren't really any people in here doing their banking day-to-days. Still, the guards seem to be working and the tellers are working kinda hard too.
  - DC 13-17: The guards are working, and they have massive wads of Gold in chests behind your desk. It's really weird that these are just out in the open. There also seems to be an area behind the teller's wall that you can go to.
  - DC 17-20: It's probably this empty because no one can get their money out. After all, you did hear earlier that the Lumination had effectively taken control of everyone's funds for their cause, and no one really seemed to care.
  - DC 20-25: Just eyeballing it, you can see 6 chests, and each chest has about 175 gold pieces in it maximum. It's a really strange amount to leave out here.
  - DC 25+ or Nat 20: The gold out the front is probably being used for "petty cash" reasons and is reserved for regular citizen transactions. It's almost guaranteed the actual gold stash is kept lower in the bank.

- To get downstairs players will need a Working Bank Rights Card and a Valid ID.
- They can find this information out by telling either of the three tellers (all elves) at the front, or try and enter via the right side passage and be told off via the guards.
  - Bank Teller 1 (left): Traelensch Barthum \- he is an eloquent older gentleman who will be kind to the players and their requests. He won't really know what players are talking about and repeatedly mentions that he's new here and will constantly get up to ask the supervisor (Kaeren).
  - Bank Teller 2 (middle): Kaeren Arguback \- she is a very feisty woman and will combat players, telling them they need a Form 32B to apply for a Working Bank Rights Card and Valid ID. She has DC 25+/Nat 20 on any speech rolls. She will ask the player for their mother's maiden name and their current closest relative's name for security and help players fill out Form 64GB for their Working Bank Rights Card and Valid ID.
  - Bank Teller 3 (right): Gruunsh'ck Diamondholder \- nerdy little Goblin, will "akshually" players as all he does is assist players in getting a Form 32B. The pipeline for this is: Form 1A (what is their chosen class?) > Form 1475L (what are their goals and ambitions in life?) > Form 69X (have they had any sexual activity in the last 45 days?) > Form 32B (Bank Teller test \- what would they do if someone tried to steal all their gold? Acceptable answers include those in the range of immediately surrendering and hitting the red button installed under all desks).

- Regarding the guards in the room…
  - They all speak the same and sound like Shax from D2.
  - If players attempt to enter dialogue with the guards and ask their purpose with an Investigation/Insight Roll:
    - DC 1-14: We're here and armed to stop all robberies and will go ahead and kill any wrongdoers.
    - DC 14-19: We aren't in the murder business. We are specially trained to detain and knock out suspicious individuals.
    - DC 19+/Nat 20: We get paid to basically stand around all the day. When we try and just talk to the citizens, they just get this stupid blank stare back. Although, the last few days, people have been a bit more chirpy.
  - Also, these guards look strong as fuck. Probably best not to mess with them, especially since there are 8 that you can see.

- PCs have a chance to take a peek the Bank Teller Desks.
  - DC 1-13: Nothing of note.
  - DC 13+: Pamphlets addressed to the Teller(s), expressing that no guard should move from their post without alerting the Tellers first with the phrase: "Visitors to Basement Floor, stand aside!". If Tellers don't hear this, they may interrogate visitors and hit their emergency lockdown button.

# Spell Scenarios

Message Spell:

- Try and catch out on the fact that the spell ends once the tasked is achieved. This may and can lead to a moment in which the NPCs completes the task, and falls away from the spell (Charmed).
- Bank Tellers have been specially trained in the art of Speech Checks / Charming. They will have high DCs, and have a \+3 to Wisdom Saving Throws.
- Otherwise \- **completely allow this to happen, baring extraneous circumstances.**

Invisibility:

- Bank Tellers and regular guards can not see this behaviour at all. It's the Order Guards downstairs (Dawnbreakers) that can Detect Magic and will roll Arcana when interacted with, and only then. They will not randomly sense the magic, but talking to a Visible PC will cause them to do an Arcana check and find the Invis PC via osmosis.
- Will allow PCs to know that Invisibility is finicky, and to have an action ready to dodge (be vague about it). Dex Saving Throw; guard will throw sword at player's Arcana.

Disguise Self:

- If a guard studies the PC, they will need to obviously roll Investigation against their Spell Save DC. No disadvantage/advantage unless the PC does something thematically sick to allow it to happen.

Minor Illusion/Prestidigitation:

- If a guard studies the PC's illusion, they will need to obviously roll Investigation against their Spell Save DC. No disadvantage/advantage unless the PC does something thematically sick to allow it to happen. However \- one of the guards will send ANOTHER ONE of the guards to investigate this; using that action there. This will basically take a guard out of the fold.

Detect Object:

- Thick leads walls, except through the bank vault.
- PC that used it, will make an Arcana Check.
  - DC Nat 1: There's no gold in there at all.
  - DC 1-7: There's something blocking your magic \- it must be a lead wall.
  - DC 7+: There's something blocking your magic, but you know Gold, and you can feel the presence of at least a metric fuckton of it. Behind this lead wall (or behind the vault door if in that room).
