## Session Prep / Direction

*Key beats, NPC dialogue notes, planned direction for the session. How it's supposed to go.*

Built for ~4hrs. Everything here already exists as an open thread - nothing new to design, just run it.

**1. Cold open - Halden's skeleton fight (~45 min)**
Fenrik + Halden vs. 8 x Skeleton (1/4 CR), door finally gives. Party's still asleep at the Den - decide beforehand whether anyone wakes up/joins mid-fight or Fenrik solos it (solo is scarier, fits the cliffhanger better, but check party balance vs. 8 skeletons before committing). Halden pays 250gp flat once it's over, very grateful. Good hard combat opener while everyone's fresh.

**2. Regroup + downtime (~20 min)**
Fenrik reunites with the party at the Den next morning. Let the table catch up out loud on where everyone stands (Kaelen's unclaimed theft, the bonds discovery, Vanya). This is cheap time that also functions as a recap for anyone who missed last session.

**3. Frah'nk & Lira Vance (~45–60 min)**
Whisper's note sends Frah'nk to the Archives. Lira has maps (Gilded Anvil/Bathhouse/Solstice Vault/Lumination Archive per the existing DC table in The Big One.md) - this is the Blueprints prep-checklist line, so resolving it here also advances the heist. Decide now: what's Whisper's promised "extra information"? Suggest tying it to the Morvault bonds (she'd have reason to know why DOL debt-paper is piling up) or to Traelensch's reliability (ties back to Rissa's Leverage thread) - pick whichever raises the stakes you want tonight.

**4. The Morvault bonds confrontation (~45–60 min)**
Multiple 25k bonds is too big to sit on. Options, pick one on the fly based on player energy: (a) party seeks Morvault out directly to ask what the bonds mean - risky, he's dangerous to provoke but the deadline pressure justifies it; (b) they dig into Vault paperwork further with Plumbodian/Gruunsh'ck for more context first; (c) Morvault initiates contact himself, reframing the session's back half around him. (a) or (c) gives you a strong combat-adjacent tension scene without an actual fight (recall: violence against him auto-defaults the bargain - keep that leash on).

**5. Vanya thread, if time allows (~30 min)**
Optional beat - B1 staff still won't talk about her. Could be a short scene (Plumbodian pushes Gruunsh'ck or another desk worker) or held for Session 10 if the above beats run long. Don't force it.

**6. Close on a hook**
Options to end on: Zatharial finally surfaces (he's overdue - could land here as a mid-heist-prep wildcard, per the Consequence Framework's "split a wedge down the middle" idea); or Ronan realizes/confronts the party about the robbery; or the heist crew (Silas/Du'geshk) pushes for a firm heist date now that blueprints + bonds intel exist. Pick whichever thread got the most energy tonight.
