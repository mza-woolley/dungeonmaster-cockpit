Session 4 \- Solvetra West ep.1

***INTRODUCTION:***

The district ahead greets you with a blend of artistry and ambition. Slate-roofed homes lean close together, framed by blossoming trees that scatter petals across the cobbles. At its center, a fountain spills clear water into the air, its gentle splash mingling with the hum of voices and the clatter of cart wheels.

Lanterns already glow along the streets, guiding you past a busy shop where crates, tools, and traveling gear spill out under striped awnings. Not far off, a tall timbered building towers above its neighbors, its windows lit warmly and its door swinging endlessly as merchants and wanderers filter through.

Further on, a cluster of workshops thrums with life: smiths pounding iron, woodcarvers setting up displays, and the faint scent of alchemical smoke drifting from a shuttered corner. To the east, a solemn spire overlooks the district, its bells marking the hours as robed figures drift inside. Beneath its shadow, scribes and artisans keep quiet shops of their own, while a row of glass-fronted stalls catches the last of the sun.
