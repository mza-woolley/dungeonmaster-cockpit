## Introduction

*A narrative recap of last session's events and a hook into this one. Written in second-person present tense - describe what the players remember and where they left off, ending on a cliffhanger or inciting moment.*

You're standing in the shadow of the Solvetra Windmill, the Lorewarden's amulet still warm in Plumbodian's pocket, a wooden windmill replica rattling in Wizzleforth's bag. Behind you: a manuscript returned (and secretly read), a halfling with a grudge, and a room upstairs where the Lorewarden let slip more than he probably meant to - that he knows about Morvault, that he knows about Valerius, that Elaris's mother might be watching closer than she lets on.

Tomorrow, at noon, right here under this windmill, Solvetra gets its new Headguard.

And underneath all of it, the real work hasn't moved: no keys in hand, no date set, no plan locked. Three sessions of prep on a vault that's still just sitting there.
