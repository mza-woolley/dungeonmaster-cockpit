## Introduction

*A narrative recap of last session's events and a hook into this one. Written in second-person present tense - describe what the players remember and where they left off, ending on a cliffhanger or inciting moment.*

Six stones, no wrong steps. You read the slab, you counted it out, and the arrows in the ceiling never got to fire. Whatever's been keeping this place sealed thought better of you than you deserve.

So you're standing in the doorway of the last room with nothing left in the tank. Kaelen is holding himself together. Nobody has slept. Behind you there's a door you never opened, and you've stopped thinking about it.

Ahead: a goblin in stolen vestments, flanked three deep, standing under a ceiling that someone once painted for a god.

And in a cage on the floor, a woman in a bank clerk's coat looks up at you and starts to cry.

Morvault's thirteen hours do not care that you are tired.

## Session Prep / Direction

*Key beats, NPC dialogue notes, planned direction for the session. How it's supposed to go.*

### Shape of the night (~4.5hr)

| Block | Time | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Yeesal fight | 60-75 min | Opens cold. No rest, no breather. |
| Vanya freed, Room 6 stripped | 30 min | |
| Travel back + sell the barrels | 45-60 min | Clock pressure, not a montage. |
| Morvault parley | 60-75 min | The climax. Non-combat. |

Do not plan a fifth block. **Room 4 (3 x Goblin Warlock) is the buffer** - drop it in only if the night runs short or the party gets through the boss too cleanly. Otherwise it stays unentered and carries forward again.

### 1. The boss fight

3 x Goblin Boss + Lightbender Yeesal, against a bloodied party with no resources. Yeesal is a *Chief Believer* - clergy, not a warlord. He should talk, and what he says is the session's main-story beat.

The reveal: **his patron is the Lorewarden Emeritus.** Entirely DM-side until now. A name said aloud mid-fight, or found on the body in the vestments afterwards.

Vanya is in the room for the whole fight. The cage is a target, a hostage, and a reason not to use the big AOE.

### 2. Vanya - the way into the Vault

She was never going to be traded, and Morvault has no interest in her anyway - to him she's a cog in the works. **She is a Vault play, full stop.** Don't run a dilemma here; run a payoff.

What she's worth:

- the only living person who has **signed for the Vault's locks**
- **Vault Key 1 is still sitting on her own desk**, unrecovered
- a witness the OOTS erased with no record trail - a living Vanya shouldn't exist
- she does not know the 4th lock exists. She managed three, signed for three, and says so with total confidence
- her routine is weeks stale, and Vasse's rotating countersign went in after her time
- too broken to be useful immediately - she recovers over time

Her pending lines, finally delivered: *"Solvetra Vault... they're taking the money"* · *"It's all the Division"*

The payoff: they came for the Vault and they got it. She can't be used immediately - too broken, recovers over time - so what they leave the ruins with is a *route*, not a solution. That's the session's forward motion into 14.

### 3. The money - the real pressure

The party is roughly **2,000gp short** of the buyout. Barrels fence at **800gp** on the third-party market - a severe discount, and everyone knows exactly why.

So **three barrels covers it** with margin. The Room 6 stores are large, and three barrels is an easy cart load.

**This means the session's tension is not the sum - it's the conversion.** They have the goods and the time is thin:

- ~13 hours on the clock, of which roughly half a day is travel
- contraband Division elixir has to be moved *into* Solvetra and offloaded fast
- the fence has to want three barrels at once, at that price, today
- Kaelen is already wanted - Ronan reported the Wayfarer's Cache robbery and the guards have beaten him once. He's the worst possible person to be moving barrels through the city, and the Session 11 halfling killings left live witnesses

Run the sale as the squeeze. If the fence lowballs, stalls, wants a cut, or the guards get involved, the shortfall is what makes Morvault's terms fire. **That's the dial** - if you want the 50% branch to happen, it happens here, not at the parley.

The remaining stock stays in Room 6 as both a future asset and evidence of the Division's distribution chain.

### 4. The parley

Run as negotiation, never a fight. Morvault is untouchable during parley - violence auto-defaults the bargain in his favour, and the fiction must telegraph that before anyone swings.

Two branches, decided by how the barrel sale went:

**They make the 5,000gp.** The buyout is honoured and the original bargain is void. Play it as cold and transactional - no warmth, no congratulation. He counts it, he leaves, and the party should feel they've bought a reprieve rather than a resolution. He deals in people and he remembers faces; "the next adventuring party" framing means he may well have made other arrangements with other people.

**They come up short.** He takes **50% of the debt now** and the remaining 50% carries as a standing debt until paid off, with interest.

The tension point is the interest clause, not the number. He states plainly that **the rate is entirely at his discretion** - no schedule, no ceiling, revisable whenever he likes. The party cannot calculate their way out of this, and that's the point. He delivers it as a courtesy he's extending.

Either way the heist is still on, because the Vault is where the Division's money is and Vanya just told them so.

Held in reserve: the *"you were always the collateral"* reveal. Adrion sold the next party he fell in with; gold was only ever the buyout clause. Worth banking even if they pay - it recontextualises a win.

### 5. Checklist coverage

- **Main-story beat** - Yeesal's patron is the Lorewarden Emeritus.
- **PC spotlight** - **Elaris.** Morvault cornered her alone on watch in Session 11; the parley is her payoff.
- **Thread advanced** - Vanya found, extracted, and established as the route back into the Vault.
- **Hook to end on** - *"It's all the Division"* points straight back at the Vault.
- **Explicitly parked** - **Zatharial.** Eight sessions pending, but stacking a second claim while the Morvault clock runs puts both in the party's face at once. Parked on purpose, not forgotten.
