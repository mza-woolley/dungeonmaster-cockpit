# Division of Lumination — Notoriety Tiers

Tracks the party's escalating notoriety with the Division of Lumination. Tiers 0–6 are triggered by completing Lunar Encounters; Tiers 7–10 are triggered by retiring OOTS guards.

| Tier | Codename | Action Required | Surveillance Status | Operational Response | Symbolic Interpretation |
|------|----------|-----------------|--------------------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| 0 | Dust Phase | Complete 5 Lunar Encounters | No awareness | No action | No lunar trace detected |
| 1 | Grey Drift | Complete 15 Lunar Encounters | Passive anomaly logs | Ignored or flagged for future review | Minor lunar interference suspected |
| 2 | Echo Bloom | Complete 25 Lunar Encounters | Recurring anomalies noted | Low-tier observers dispatched | Echoes of lunar myth in behaviour |
| 3 | Symbol Sheen | Complete 35 Lunar Encounters | Symbols traced to party movement | Local informants activated | Lunar ideology leaking into populace |
| 4 | Vein Wax | Complete 45 Lunar Encounters | Party linked to ideological spread | Regional containment protocols triggered | Considered carriers of lunar contagion |
| 5 | Pulse Gibbous | Complete 55 Lunar Encounters | Party seen as nexus of mythic activity | Division agents embedded nearby | Lunar resonance destabilising local order |
| 6 | Rite Halo | Complete 65 Lunar Encounters | Belief systems traced to party influence | Propaganda and discrediting campaigns launched | Lunar myth seen as conscious ideology |
| 7 | Crescent Fault | Kill/retire 1 × OOTS | Party actions interpreted as systemic threat | Tactical suppression teams mobilised | Lunar progression seen as deliberate rebellion |
| 8 | Eclipse Thorn | Kill/retire 2 × OOTS | Party classified as existential disruptors | High-level Division strategists engaged | Lunar myth threatens solar supremacy |
| 9 | Myth Core | Kill/retire 4 × OOTS | Party believed to generate new myth | Division fractures internally over response | Lunar truth seen as self-propagating force |
| 10 | Moonwake Singularity | Kill/retire 7 × OOTS | Surveillance abandoned — myth cannot be contained | Division collapses or mutates | Party has now toppled the Division |
