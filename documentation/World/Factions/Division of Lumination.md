# Division of Lumination (DIL)

The public, administrative face of power in Le'Choix. Rose to fill the power vacuum left when Tre Illium collapsed and the Shadowsong attacked. On the surface, the DIL runs the ministries, controls financial institutions (the Solvetra Vault), curates the historical record, and projects soft power through cities like Ars Pontaris. Citizens aren't particularly hard done by under it - the DIL isn't necessarily malevolent, it's simply the skewed, generational top of a feudalist-shaped society.

**What the DIL actually is:** the administrative front the **Order of the Sun (OOTS)** operates through. The ministries, the lantern-bearing agent network, the financial control - all of it is the legitimate-looking face that lets OOTS's real doctrinal and power-brokerage engine run underneath, using sol-worship and "light" ideology to keep expanding its reach over the nation. See [Order of the Sun](Order%20of%20the%20Sun.md).

The Division actively suppresses lunar ideology and rewrites historical memory - not out of bureaucratic paranoia, but because every surviving lunar relic, forgotten shrine, or Shadowsong sympathizer is a fuse that could relight the Lunar Accord and unravel the DIL's founding claim that light-without-shadow is the correct order.

**Key operatives:**
- **The Order of the Sun (OOTS)** - the doctrinal and power-brokerage engine underneath the DIL's administrative face. See its own file for structure and roster.
- **Lorewarden Emeritus** - Archive figure connected to the DIL. Has ties to the Lumina Lab and the Solvetra Vault.
- **Valerius du Noirveil (deceased)** - Former Headguard of Solvetra West. Admitted to sending hired thugs. Defeated by the party in the Lumina Lab cave.
