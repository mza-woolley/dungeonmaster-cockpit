# The Shadowbringers

A rebellion organisation working to dissolve the Division of Lumination. Operates out of **Smuggler's Den** in Solvetra Intermellum.

**Key members:**
- **Silas Shadowfoot** (Male, Halfling) - Main quest giver. Short, brown-haired, grey/black scout clothing; bow and quiver. Leader of the Shadowbringer Deliverers.
- **Silvanus Shadowfoot** (Silas's father) - Former Dawnbringer who broke his oath after a thieving ambush took his leg and wife. Now an Oathbreaker Paladin walking the Path of the Shadowbringer.
- **Corwin Duskwatch** (Male, Half-Goblin/Orc, Cleric) - Small with fangs. Devout follower of Shar.
- **Du'geshk Maharjuuj** (Female, Half-Orc/Half-Dragonborn, Fighter) - Buff, greatsword and heavy armour. Heading the Heist Operation.
- **Chasley Fanatirela** (Female, High Elf, Sorcerer) - Very posh.
- **Venn & Varr** (Male, Unknown Race, Way of the Kensei Monks) - Silent. Traumatised, don't speak. Extremely loyal to Silas.
