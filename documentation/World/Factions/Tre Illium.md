# Tre Illium (Illuminare, Illuminari)

The three founders of Le Dernier Choix. Operated under strict secrecy - their true identities are still unknown to the general public. Known to have established the region with a vision of lunar-solar balance, but their rule was eventually undermined by both the Shadowsong and the newly forming Division of Lumination.

The three members of Tre Illium:
- **Kaela Shadowsong** - ⅓ of Tre Illium; the savioress of La'Choix and the Bringer of the Shadowsilence. A Shadowsong who turned against her lineage. Possessor of the Orb.
- **Sibling Thorne** - ⅓ of Tre Illium; the Angel of Light, the Vassal of Light. Created out of pure divine solar energy to stop the Shadowsong from corrupting the nation.
- **Myxtiferious** - ⅓ of Tre Illium; the Red Dragon Lord. Rode through La'Choix bringing burning illumination to Shadowsong practitioners. Awarded *Lux Invicta* to rule until the end of his bloodline, in return vowing to never return to his draconic form.

Tre Illium were working toward a **lunar outcome** - a perfect symbiosis of night and light that would satisfy all power holders in La'Choix. This idea lost steam, citizens forgot the lunar ideology, but effigies and symbolism remained throughout the world.
