# The Order of the Sun (OOTS)

The real power-brokerage engine of Le'Choix, wearing the **Division of Lumination** as its administrative front. Where the DIL is ministries, financial control, and curated history, the OOTS is what actually harnesses sol-doctrine and light-worship to keep expanding its grip over the nation - detaining rather than killing, rewriting memory rather than debating it, and treating "the Order" the way a family treats tradition.

Armour bears a sun sigil. Based in the lower floors of the Solvetra Vault.

---

## Head of the Order

**Virelle** sits at the top of the OOTS - not the DIL in name, though she's nominally supreme over both since the DIL is the OOTS's own front. She stopped actively steering the Order once it became clear the institution was never the point for her; her real goal is **light-godhood**, full ascension beyond the touch Sibling Thorne gave her. She is indifferent to what her underlings do with the Order's power, as long as it keeps running. See [Virelle](../../NPCs/Villains/Virelle.md).

That absence is why the OOTS feels the way the party has experienced it - inconsistent, personally driven, every operator running their own angle. As long as an operator holds power *of the front* - the DIL's legitimacy, resources, and reach - they keep pursuing their own ambitions under the Order's banner, unchecked from above.
