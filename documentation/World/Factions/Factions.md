# Factions of Le'Choix

## Tre Illium (Illuminare, Illuminari)

The three founders of Le Dernier Choix. Operated under strict secrecy — their true identities are still unknown to the general public. Known to have established the region with a vision of lunar-solar balance, but their rule was eventually undermined by both the Shadowsong and the newly forming Division of Lumination.

The three members of Tre Illium:
- **Kaela Shadowsong** — ⅓ of Tre Illium; the savioress of La'Choix and the Bringer of the Shadowsilence. A Shadowsong who turned against her lineage. Possessor of the Orb.
- **Sibling Thorne** — ⅓ of Tre Illium; the Angel of Light, the Vassal of Light. Created out of pure divine solar energy to stop the Shadowsong from corrupting the nation.
- **Myxtiferious** — ⅓ of Tre Illium; the Red Dragon Lord. Rode through La'Choix bringing burning illumination to Shadowsong practitioners. Awarded *Lux Invicta* to rule until the end of his bloodline, in return vowing to never return to his draconic form.

Tre Illium were working toward a **lunar outcome** — a perfect symbiosis of night and light that would satisfy all power holders in La'Choix. This idea lost steam, citizens forgot the lunar ideology, but effigies and symbolism remained throughout the world.

## Division of Lumination (DIL)

The ruling autocracy of Le'Choix. Rose to power following the fall of Tre Illium, filling the power vacuum left when the Shadowsong attacked. The DIL are not necessarily malevolent — citizens aren't particularly hard done by — but the power balance is firmly skewed to the top and is now generational.

The Division actively suppresses lunar ideology, rewrites historical memory, controls financial institutions, and monitors the populace through a network of lantern-bearing agents and the **Order of the Sun (OOTS)**.

**Key operatives:**
- **The Order of the Sun (OOTS)** — Elite guard force. Armour bears a sun sigil. Based in Solvetra Vault.
- **Lorewarden Emeritus** — Archive figure connected to the DOL. Has ties to the Lumina Lab and the vault.
- **Valerius du Noirveil (deceased)** — Former Headguard of Solvetra West. Admitted to sending hired thugs. Defeated by the party in the Lumina Lab cave.

## The Shadowsong (Shadowsingers)

The Drow lineage who attempted to overthrow Tre Illium. Their power came from the **Shadowrealm** — a locked dimension containing chaotic dark magic that was used to try to bring about the end of times in La'Choix.

After being crushed and snuffed out by the Division of Lumination, they were effectively eliminated as an active force. However, the threat of the Shadowrealm remains — the Orb is the "lock" to the Shadowrealm. If the Shadowsong obtained it, they could destroy it and unleash ultimate power.

---

## The Shadowbringers

A rebellion organisation working to dissolve the Division of Lumination. Operates out of **Smuggler's Den** in Solvetra Intermellum.

**Key members:**
- **Silas Shadowfoot** (Male, Halfling) — Main quest giver. Short, brown-haired, grey/black scout clothing; bow and quiver. Leader of the Shadowbringer Deliverers.
- **Silvanus Shadowfoot** (Silas's father) — Former Dawnbringer who broke his oath after a thieving ambush took his leg and wife. Now an Oathbreaker Paladin walking the Path of the Shadowbringer.
- **Corwin Duskwatch** (Male, Half-Goblin/Orc, Cleric) — Small with fangs. Devout follower of Shar.
- **Du'geshk Maharjuuj** (Female, Half-Orc/Half-Dragonborn, Fighter) — Buff, greatsword and heavy armour. Heading the Heist Operation.
- **Chasley Fanatirela** (Female, High Elf, Sorcerer) — Very posh.
- **Venn & Varr** (Male, Unknown Race, Way of the Kensei Monks) — Silent. Traumatised, don't speak. Extremely loyal to Silas.

---

## Northern Star of the Primordial Fist (NSPF)

A military order, funded and bankrolled by the Division of Lumination. Broke up Fenrik and Sylphie; also came after Fenrik's wife and children. Played a role in toppling the Royal Dragon Regency — the power, area, and throne once held by Myxtiferious.

Their leader: **Aurevon Beitomy Erstua**. Their goons will kill themselves before admitting to anything under interrogation.

---

## The Order of the Sun (OOTS)

The Division's elite enforcement arm. Guards bear a sun sigil on their armour. They are specially trained to detain rather than kill, follow strict protocols, and react to any deviation from their process. Stationed within the Solvetra Vault's lower floors.
