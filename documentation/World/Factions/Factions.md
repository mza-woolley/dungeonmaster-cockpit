# Factions of Le'Choix

- [Tre Illium](Tre%20Illium.md) - the three founders of Le Dernier Choix; the collapsed Lunar Accord
- [Division of Lumination](Division%20of%20Lumination.md) - the DIL, the public administrative front of power in Le'Choix
- [Order of the Sun](Order%20of%20the%20Sun.md) - the OOTS, the real power-brokerage engine underneath the DIL
- [The Shadowsong](The%20Shadowsong.md) - the Drow lineage crushed after their bid to overthrow Tre Illium
- [The Shadowbringers](The%20Shadowbringers.md) - the rebellion working to dissolve the Division of Lumination
- [Northern Star of the Primordial Fist](Northern%20Star%20of%20the%20Primordial%20Fist.md) - the DIL-funded military order (NSPF)
