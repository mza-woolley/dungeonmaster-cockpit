# Adrion's Devil Contract

**Status:** Active  
**Key Figure:** Zatharial (Devil)  
**Established:** Session 4

---

## Overview

In Solvetra West, Zatharial offered Adrion the ability to teleport to his party in exchange for signing a contract. Adrion signed without reading it.

## Contract Terms

Zatharial may claim **Adrion's soul and the souls of the party** upon Adrion's death.

## Current State

- The party may not all be aware of the full terms
- Zatharial has not yet acted on the contract
- He has a stated interest in keeping the party alive — he believes they are the ones who will defeat the Light Vassal — but this protection is conditional and self-serving

## Potential Use

- Zatharial can appear to remind Adrion (or the party) of the contract at any time
- Intended as a wedge to drive between Adrion and the group when dramatically appropriate
- Could be leveraged if the party pushes too far against Zatharial's interests
