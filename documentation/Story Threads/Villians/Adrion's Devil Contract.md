# Adrion's Devil Contract

**Status:** Active — contract intact, not yet resolved  
**Key Figure:** Zatharial (Devil)  
**Established:** Session 4  
**Planned Resolution:** Zatharial tears up the contract — planned for Session 8, did not happen, still pending

---

## Overview

See [Adrion](../../Companions/Adrion.md) for his background and current status (former PC, now NPC).

In Solvetra West, Zatharial offered Adrion the ability to teleport to his party in exchange for signing a contract. Adrion signed without reading it. The contract remains active and unresolved — the party's souls are currently bound by it, alongside Adrion's.

## Original Contract Terms

Zatharial can claim **Adrion's soul and the souls of the party** upon Adrion's death.

## Current State

- Contract is **active** — the party's souls are bound, alongside Adrion's, right now
- This runs in parallel with [Lucien Morvault](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Lucien%20Morvault.md)'s active claim on the party from Session 7 — two live claims on the table simultaneously, both still live through Session 8
- Zatharial has not yet appeared before the remaining party members regarding this

## Planned: The Tearing

Was planned for Session 8; didn't happen. Still an open hook for whenever it lands. Zatharial appears before the party and tears the contract in front of them — not as an act of mercy, but as a demonstration of power. The party should come away knowing he had leverage over them and chose to release it. That choice should be deliberate and self-serving. He wants them to feel the weight of it — they now owe him something, even though nothing more was signed.

- What prompts Zatharial to finally appear — is it triggered by Adrion's status, the fallout of the vault heist, or his own timing?
- Does the reveal that the party's souls were on the line *this whole time* land before or after he tears it up?
- How does this interact with the Morvault claim — does Zatharial know about it? Could he use it as leverage in this same scene?
