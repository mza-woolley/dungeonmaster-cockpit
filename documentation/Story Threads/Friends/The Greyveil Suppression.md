# The Greyveil Suppression — Story Thread

**Status:** Dormant — available to activate  
**Entry Point:** Frah'nk Frapper joining the party; Solvetra East exploration  
**Key Figures:** Frah'nk Frapper, Silvanus Shadowfoot, Memory Archivist's Vault, Index of Absence

---

## The Hook

The party is now travelling with someone who was there. Frah'nk doesn't bring it up unprompted — it's not something he performs — but if the party spends time in Solvetra East, visits the right buildings, or asks the right questions, the thread opens naturally.

Solvetra East doesn't look like a place that was taken by force. That's the point. It looks clean, institutional, Order-aligned. The violence has been replaced by paperwork and architecture. But the bones of what it was are still there for anyone who knows how to look.

---

## Where the Evidence Lives

**Index of Absence — Solvetra East (Library)**
The shelves were cleared "by decree of the Curator of Memory." The Curator is an Order title. What was removed almost certainly included records of Greyveil — the name, the population, the events. The quest hook is already active: *recover the forbidden texts before the purge completes.* If the party gets there in time, a fragment of what Greyveil was could still exist.

**Memory Archivist's Vault — Solvetra Intermellum**
The Intermellum is where things the Order couldn't fully destroy ended up hidden. The Memory Archivist's Vault is explicitly a repository of things that shouldn't exist. Greyveil records — names, a census, a firsthand account — could be buried here. The Archivist may not even know what they're holding.

**Frah'nk Frapper**
He was there. He can describe it. If the party finds a fragment of evidence and brings it to him, or if they're standing in Solvetra East and he's with them, this is a moment. He doesn't need to be dramatic about it — matter-of-fact is worse.

**Silvanus Shadowfoot**
Also there. Carries more guilt about it than Frah'nk does, or at least wears it differently. If the party confronts him with evidence, or if Frah'nk and Silvanus are in the same room when it comes up, there's something to play there.

---

## What It Means

The Greyveil Suppression isn't just backstory for Frah'nk and Silvanus. It's proof of something the party has only seen the edges of so far — that the DOL doesn't just suppress and control, it *erases*. Solvetra East is the most complete example of what they're capable of, and it's been sitting in plain sight the whole time.

If the party exposes it — publishes a record, confronts the Order publicly, or gets the story to the right people — it could shift how Solvetra's citizens see the Division. Not just as oppressive, but as the force that destroyed their own city's history and replaced it with a lie.
