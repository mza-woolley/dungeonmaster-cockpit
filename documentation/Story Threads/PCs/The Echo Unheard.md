# The Echo Unheard

**Status:** Dormant since Session 5 - proposed resurfacing point: Session 7 (Solvetra Vault)
**Key Figures:** Ne'leak Shadowsong (Bardic Cult), Kaelen (Måneklang)
**Established:** Session 5 - The False Stage

---

## Overview

Måneklang revealed the name "The Echo Unheard" when Kaelen used it to evaporate the bards at The False Stage - performers belonging to Ne'leak Shadowsong's bardic cult. Nothing has followed up on this since. See [The Echo Unheard](../History%20&%20Lore/The%20Echo%20Unheard.md) and [Måneklang](../../Loot%20&%20Magic%20Items/Story%20Items/Maneklang.md).

---

## Proposed Hook for Session 7 - The Vault

The Solvetra Vault is built into a cave system with a river running beneath the lower floors - old stone, older than the bank built on top of it. Suggestion: somewhere in the Vault Room or B1 (perhaps near the sewer/river grate entry point), Kaelen - and only Kaelen, only while carrying Måneklang - notices a faint resonant hum coming from the foundation itself. Måneklang doesn't react violently this time; it just... listens.

If Kaelen investigates (Perception/Investigation, or simply by getting close to the source), he can sense the hum is the *same frequency* that triggered "The Echo Unheard" at The False Stage - but older, and coming from beneath the city rather than from the bardic cult's instruments. The cult didn't create this resonance; they were tuning into something that was already there.

This doesn't need to be resolved in Session 7 - a single sensory beat (Kaelen's the only one who notices, the others can see he's gone quiet/distracted) is enough to keep the thread alive without derailing the heist. It plants:

- The Echo Unheard predates Ne'leak Shadowsong's cult - possibly Tre Illium era, possibly older
- It's tied to *place*, not just to Måneklang or the cult - something is buried/sealed beneath Solvetra
- A future thread: is this connected to the Shadowrealm gate / Lux Infracta the Orb is attuned to? The vault sits on a river over old stone - worth considering whether this is geographically or thematically linked to the lunar/Shadowsong threads running through the Plumbodian/Kaelen lineage arcs

## Notes

- Keep this subtle - a single line of flavour text during the heist is enough. Over-explaining kills the mystery.
- If the party doesn't bite, the hum can resurface later (Ars Lapis' Echo Veins - "the stones sing" - is a suspiciously on-the-nose location to revisit this, if you want a second touchpoint)
