# Wizzleforth's Lineage Vision

**Type:** Story Thread - Personal Arc / Lore  
**Status:** Partially revealed - the twist is unresolved

---

## What Happened

During Session 5, while studying the Division of Lumination's texts at the Lumination Archive, Wizzleforth experienced two things:

- A **form of astral projection** - at the time framed as tied to his own self-lineage
- A **journey back in time** - the nature and content of which is not yet fully documented

---

## The Twist

The vision is real - it just isn't Wizzleforth's. He has no connection to the Tre Illium/Plumbodian/Kaela lineage at all (he's an isolated old DOL cleric - see [PC Family Arcs](../../Players/Party%20Overview/PC%20Family%20Arcs.md)). What he experienced actually belongs to **Kaelen** - a glimpse into the Shadowsong lineage Kaelen is a distant, loose descendant of, surfacing in the wrong person's head.

---

## Notes

- Wizzleforth studied the Division of Lumination's texts extensively in Sessions 5–6, gaining WIS, INT, and Religion proficiency by Session 7 - he's been piecing together a story that isn't about him, which could make for a great "wait, that's not my family" moment when the truth surfaces.
- The mechanism for *why* Kaelen's vision happened in Wizzleforth's head is TBD - could be proximity, could be tied to Måneklang, could be deliberate (someone/something misdirecting it).
- The time-travel element is unresolved - what was witnessed, and how/when it gets correctly attributed to Kaelen, is TBD.
- This thread connects directly to the Tre Illium arc and the lunar goal - just through Kaelen, not Wizzleforth.
