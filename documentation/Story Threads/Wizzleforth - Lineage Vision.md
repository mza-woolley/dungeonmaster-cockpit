# Wizzleforth's Lineage Vision

**Type:** Story Thread — Personal Arc / Lore  
**Status:** Partially revealed — implications unresolved

---

## What Happened

During Session 5, while studying the Division of Lumination's texts at the Lumination Archive, Wizzleforth experienced two things:

- A **form of astral projection** tied to his self-lineage — a vision rooted in who and what he comes from
- A **journey back in time** — the nature and content of which is not yet fully documented

---

## Lineage Relevance

Wizzleforth's family tree is far more significant than he knows. Key points from the [PC Family Arcs](../Players/Party%20Overview/PC%20Family%20Arcs.md):

- His father **Plumbodian the 4th** carries **false memories** implanted by Wizzleforth's mother, **Virelle** — she erased his recollection of her and their child, sinking the Armada of Glimmerdeep with the last of her power to cover her tracks
- Wizzleforth has a half-sibling, **Elaris**, who is sun-blessed (Aasimar) and unknowingly patroned by **Sibling Thorne** — the Vassal of Light
- The **Orb** that Plumbodian carries traces back to **Kaela Shadowsong**, ⅓ of Tre Illium — Wizzleforth's magical lineage runs directly through the founding of Le'Choix

The astral projection may represent Wizzleforth beginning to sense the weight of this lineage — the false memories, Virelle's sacrifice, and the Orb's true origin.
