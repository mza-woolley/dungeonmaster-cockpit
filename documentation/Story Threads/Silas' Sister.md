# Silas' Sister — The Missing Shadowfoot

**Type:** Story Thread — Missing Person / Backstory  
**Status:** Unresolved

---

## Overview

During the thieving ambush that cost **Silvanus Shadowfoot** his leg and his wife, the thieves also kidnapped his daughter — Silas' sister. She was never found dead or alive.

---

## Known Facts

- Kidnapped in the same ambush that killed Silvanus' wife and severed his leg
- No body was ever recovered — she is neither confirmed dead nor confirmed alive
- Silvanus lost faith in the Division/Order following this event and became an Oathbreaker Paladin
- Silas has never mentioned her to the party directly; this was learned through Silvanus

---

## Notes

- Silvanus expressed a hint of sadness when recounting the event — this is clearly not a closed chapter for him
- Her fate is entirely open — she could be imprisoned, in hiding, working for the Division, or worse
- Silas' motivations for leading the Shadowbringers may be partly rooted in this loss
