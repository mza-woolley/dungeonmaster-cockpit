# Herb Collection

### Wayfarer's Cache
**Location:** Wayfarer's Cache | **NPC:** Ronan Emberlain 

(Loud and cheerful, sells everything from rope to rations out of crates spilling onto the street under striped awnings - the type to upsell a "lucky" whetstone with a straight face.)

**Hook:** Flagged as the party's next step at the end of Session 7, following Plumbodian's B1 infiltration. Pay rate for the job amended to 200gp.

**Objective:** ~~Ronan needs a herb supply run - pick up a batch of herbs for him.~~ **Resolved, Session 8.**

**Status:** Complete. Herbs confirmed as chamomile tea. Party paid out, plus took a cut of shop stock as a "danger fee" after the Banshee ambush.

---
Log:

- PCs need money, and Ronan has work. He needs someone to go  pick some herbs for him.
- The herbs are to be picked just outside of the city, in the dark part of the forest around Solvetra West (north).
- As they pick, a Banshee will appear and effectively lurk. If they don't think about killing it, it shouldn't instantly attack - will make it be ominous and lurk.
- **Session 8:** Elaris, Frah'nk, Fenrik, and Wizzleforth went north to the clearing to collect the herbs - confirmed as chamomile tea. The Banshee surfaced; Frah'nk sensed it was Undead, and Wizzleforth's Turn Undead failed (Banshee made its save). Fenrik covered its head with a sack before the fight broke out; the party defeated it but took heavy damage. They returned to Ronan with the sack, the chamomile, and the Banshee's head.
- Back at the Cache, the party argued a "danger fee" over the unexpected Banshee fight - Ronan agreed and let them take stock from the shop on top of payment. (This happened while Ronan was separately dealing with having just been robbed by Kaelen - see [Ronan Emberlain](../../../../NPCs/Neutral/Solvetra/Solvetra%20West/Ronan%20Emberlain.md).)
