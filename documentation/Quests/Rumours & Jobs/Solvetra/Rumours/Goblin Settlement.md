# Goblin Settlement

**Location:** Outside Solvetra East, roughly half a day's trek toward Ars Lapis
**Status:** Party-initiated raid, ready when Kaelen bites. Low-stakes, hack-and-slash.

---

## Premise

Kaelen wants the nest that bred the goblin who killed Hoogle the First (already dead - this isn't about that goblin, it's about burning out where he came from). Word of the Lab's destruction has likely reached this camp, so they're on edge/bracing - not ambushed, just jumpy.

---

## Questions to answer before mapping it

1. **How do they find it?** - stumble on it travelling to Ars Lapis (simplest), or a hook/rumour points them there?

PCs should find the Goblin Camp on the way to Ars Lapis, as a means of Hoogle the 2nd sniffing out a scent/lead to the Goblin Camp. If Hoogle in unavailable, then Plumbodian should be able to hear something like a Goblin. The general idea is that there will be a 3-man Goblin ambush in the planning, and then obviously on-the-road, they get jumped by Goblins.

In the dying moments of the fight, one Goblin should say to the other - "You need to warn the camp!".

**Alternate/earlier trigger (Session 9+):** Rissa "Whisper" Varn can point the party at this camp directly as a deliberate ~1-day side trip, ahead of any eventual Ars Lapis travel - this is the "extra information" she promised Frah'nk in Session 8. The hook: **Vanya Windros** is being held here. She wasn't cooperative with the OOTS's initiative at the Vault, so she was quietly displaced and is now stashed at the camp - ties directly into the missing-manager thread and Vault Key 1. Doesn't replace the Hoogle-scent/ambush version above; if the party hasn't gone yet by the time they travel to Ars Lapis, that version can still trigger as the fallback.

2. **How big is the camp?** - roughly how many goblins total, rough layout (just sentries / huts / chief's area is enough)?

The camp is approximately 18 Goblins large, with them occupying an old lunar fortress.

3. **Who's the final fight?** - chief, shaman, or both?

The final fight will be with a Goblin Chief Warlock and 4 x goons. Should be a custom stat-block, as "Goblin Chief Believer - Lightbender Yeesal". Their patron should be a member of the OOTS, possibly the Lorewarden.

4. **Kaelen's closure item** - what's the Hoogle the First trophy (collar, pelt, chain) and where does it live in the camp?

There will be a bunch of caged Wargs that have cut marks, etc, that Kaelen can ultimately free if he likes. Fulfilment of the quest/clearing is in the eye of the beholder (Kaelen). This expedition is of course a form of a revenge arc, but it's also so PCs can have a bit of powertrip and enjoy killing goblins.

5. **DOL ties or not?** - does this camp share the "Order of the Sun" alignment (loose thread for later), or is it fully independent (clean, self-contained)?

There are OOTS ties: the patron of the Goblin Chief Believer is the Lorewarden, and the camp is now also where the OOTS quietly stashed **Vanya Windros** after she stopped cooperating with the Vault's initiative. Also, there should be a bunch of Lumina Elixir here that is in carts and ready to be shipped to Ars Lapis, and labelled as such. There should be 3 carts, 1 for each of the Gem Families of Ars Lapis. This acts as a consequential reveal, that the PCs did not actually really stop the production, or at least, it's way way more than they could've conceived.

## World Effect: Before vs. After the Vault Heist

Vanya's rescue doesn't gate any keys - Key 1 sits unrecovered on her desk in Solvetra Vault regardless of whether she's personally freed. Her rescue is decoupled from the heist mechanically; what changes is world-state framing depending on timing. Vanya does **not** know about Key 4/the Lorewarden - even as Manager she only ever administered 3 locks, so this holds either way and the Key 4 surprise is safe.

**If the camp is hit before the heist** (Rissa/Session 9+ trigger):
- Elixir production/Ars Lapis distribution is confirmed before the vault attempt - raises the party's motivation/urgency going in, but changes nothing mechanically.
- The DOL's quiet-purge tactic (erasing an uncooperative staffer, zero record trail) is exposed pre-heist - sharpens the party's read on how ruthless the DOL's Solvetra operation is before they commit to robbing it.
- Pushes the campaign timeline: revenge-arc and heist-prep now run in parallel rather than sequentially.
- DOL/OOTS have no idea the camp was hit yet - no compounding heat, a clean isolated strike with no bearing on vault security.

**If the camp is hit after the heist** (slow burn into Ars Lapis):
- Whatever the Four Outcomes state is (DOL alert level, whether the party's identified, Shadowbringers' position) is already locked in - the raid doesn't feed back into the heist's outcome at all.
- The elixir-carts reveal lands as a world-state correction: no matter how the heist went, the DOL's actual production/distribution scale was never meaningfully dented - deflates any "we won" read on the heist.
- If the party is already burned/exposed from the heist, hitting a Lorewarden-patronized camp so soon after is heightened risk - DOL attention is already up, and this is another visible strike tied to them.
- The DOL's purge tactic confirms only as historical cleanup at this point, not as intel shaping decisions still ahead.
