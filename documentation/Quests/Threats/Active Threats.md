## Lucien Morvault
**Target:** The party — they are the actual collateral, not just debtors  
**Threat:** Non-combat. If unpaid, Lucien collects on the original bargain (the party itself, via a binding claim) — not a fight. Attacking him auto-defaults the bargain in his favour.  
**Urgency:** Critical — hard deadline, Session 7  
**Last Known Action:** Session 8 — going through Solvetra Vault bond paperwork, Plumbodian and the party confirmed multiple 25,000gp bonds addressed directly to Lucien. Frah'nk also told Rissa "Whisper" Varn that Lucien is who the party owes, and she confirmed he's bad news.  
**Next Trigger:** Deadline passes with no payment, or the party attacks during the parley  
**Notes:** Adrion's real "agreement" was sacrificing the next adventuring party (the PCs) to Morvault in exchange for his own escape after his village burned. The 5,000gp is a buyout clause, not the original debt — see [Lucien Morvault](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Lucien%20Morvault.md). Run this as a negotiation/parley, never a fight. The 25,000gp bonds discovered in Session 8 may indicate his reach is broader than the original letter implied.

## Valerius' Replacement
**Target:** Not the party specifically — see Notes  
**Threat:** Unknown — but Valerius was Headguard and this person was already organised before he died  
**Urgency:** High — timeline unknown, but the Lumina Lab's personnel were wiped out and the vault heist is imminent  
**Last Known Action:** Referenced in letter on Valerius' body: *"I've already organised your replacement."* Session 7: Tailendra's newspaper confirms a new Headguard arrives in 2 days — not yet chosen/announced publicly. Session 9: newspaper confirms the public reveal — announcement underneath the Solvetra Windmill (Middle Solvetra) at noon, 24 hours out.  
**Next Trigger:** Headguard revealed publicly at the Windmill announcement — DOL learns the vault has been robbed, or identifies the party by name  
**Notes:** Identity unknown. Likely arrives in or around Ars Lapis. Reframed: the replacement is a vanilla DOL drone — a step up from Valerius in procedure and rigour, not in cunning or politics. He's not the mole's handler and has no awareness of intelligence assets (that runs through L.E. directly). The DOL already expected a Shadowbringer move (see The Lorewarden Emeritus, below) — this replacement's brief is to tighten security and counter/contain that anticipated operation (the vault) through stricter procedure, not to hunt the party by name. The party is at risk by *association* with the Shadowbringers, not yet personally. The DOL's overall posture is "contain quietly" — restaffing the Lab and slotting in this replacement are routine internal fixes, not a visible crackdown. See [The Shadowbringer Mole](../../In%20Progress/The%20Shadowbringer%20Mole.md) and [Solvetra Headguard Replacement](../../In%20Progress/Solvetra%20Headguard%20Replacement.md).

**Urgency:** Low-Medium — contract is live but Zatharial hasn't acted on it yet  
**Last Known Action:** None yet — Zatharial has not appeared before the party regarding the contract since Session 4. Did not surface in Session 8 either — still pending.  
**Next Trigger:** Zatharial appears and tears up the contract publicly, a power move dressed as a gesture of goodwill — carried forward, not yet played  
**Notes:** Runs in parallel with Lucien Morvault's claim — two live claims on the party simultaneously through Session 7. Zatharial believes the party are destined to defeat the Light Vassal. Operates independently of DOL and Shadowbringers. His Session 8 move will come with a smile and a proposition. **Target:** Not actively hunting — but a collision is coming

**Urgency:** Low-Medium — becomes critical the moment the heist begins

**Next Trigger:** The vault is robbed and Key 4 becomes necessary — or Plumbodian's quest puts him at odds with the party  
**Notes:** Signed the Lumina Elixir distribution map as *L.E.* Received a letter from a dying DOL spy: *"The Shadowbringers, my lord — I've been had."* This letter predates the Lumina Lab raid (Session 5, "Missing Guest" quest) — L.E. already had a source watching the Shadowbringers before Session 6/7. His awareness of the party's involvement in the Lab is unconfirmed. See [The Shadowbringer Mole](../../In%20Progress/The%20Shadowbringer%20Mole.md).
