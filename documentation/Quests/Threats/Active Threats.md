## Lucien Morvault
**Target:** The party - they are the actual collateral, not just debtors  
**Threat:** Non-combat. If unpaid, Lucien collects on the original bargain (the party itself, via a binding claim) - not a fight. Attacking him auto-defaults the bargain in his favour.  
**Urgency:** CRITICAL - **~14 hours remaining as of the end of Session 12 (~13 on leaving the ruins)**  
**Last Known Action:** **Session 11** - Lucien appeared in person for the first time since the bargain, stepping out of a portal into the party's forest camp on the road to the Goblin Settlement. He caught **Elaris alone on watch** (Kaelen and Plumbodian were off scouting the chateau, caught in bear traps) and threatened her intensely - the party dies if the money isn't produced. The party reconvened and set the remaining window at roughly **18 hours**. As of the end of Session 12 they are still underground in the goblin ruins with **~14 hours left (~13 by the time they leave)** - Yeesal unfought, Vanya not yet freed, no money in hand, and roughly half a day's travel back to Solvetra.  
**Next Trigger:** The 18 hours expire with no payment, or the party attacks during the parley  
**Notes:** Adrion's real "agreement" was sacrificing the next adventuring party (the PCs) to Morvault in exchange for his own escape after his village burned. The 5,000gp is a buyout clause, not the original debt - see [Lucien Morvault](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Lucien%20Morvault.md). Run this as a negotiation/parley, never a fight. The 25,000gp bonds discovered in Session 8 may indicate his reach is broader than the original letter implied. **Session 11 note:** isolating Elaris was deliberate - he moved the moment the party split. He is now also the *reason* the party is raiding the Goblin Settlement: they want the missing bank manager as leverage/currency against this debt, and chose that over continuing heist prep.

## Valerius' Replacement - Dorian Vasse
**Target:** Not the party specifically - see Notes  
**Threat:** Headguard Dorian Vasse - promoted from internal audit, not the streets. Vanilla DOL drone: procedural, rule-abiding, genuinely loyal to the DOL mission, no political cunning. Tightened Vault security: daily rotating countersign on B1/B2 sign-in, increased fixed-schedule Vault Room patrols, and a post-incident sign-in log review if the duress alarm ever trips.  
**Urgency:** High - timeline unknown, but the Lumina Lab's personnel were wiped out and the vault heist is imminent  
**Last Known Action:** Referenced in letter on Valerius' body: *"I've already organised your replacement."* Session 7: Tailendra's newspaper confirms a new Headguard arrives in 2 days - not yet chosen/announced publicly. Session 9: newspaper confirms the public reveal - announcement underneath the Solvetra Windmill (Middle Solvetra) at noon, 24 hours out.  
**Next Trigger:** Headguard revealed publicly at the Windmill announcement - DOL learns the vault has been robbed, or identifies the party by name  
**Notes:** Identity confirmed - Dorian Vasse. Reframed: the replacement is a vanilla DOL drone - a step up from Valerius in procedure and rigour, not in cunning or politics. He's not the mole's handler and has no awareness of intelligence assets (that runs through L.E. directly). The DOL already expected a Shadowbringer move (see The Lorewarden Emeritus, below) - this replacement's brief is to tighten security and counter/contain that anticipated operation (the vault) through stricter procedure, not to hunt the party by name. The party is at risk by *association* with the Shadowbringers, not yet personally. The DOL's overall posture is "contain quietly" - restaffing the Lab and slotting in this replacement are routine internal fixes, not a visible crackdown. Vasse's daily countersign is the specific procedural gotcha that threatens Kaelen's Valerius disguise. See [Lorewarden Emeritus](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Lorewarden%20Emeritus.md) and [Solvetra Headguard Replacement](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Dorian%20Vasse.md).

## The Goblin Settlement
**Target:** The settlement (the party strikes first)  
**Threat:** ~18 goblins occupying an old chateau/lunar fortress, plus the complex beneath it  
**Urgency:** **In progress - the raid is happening right now, mid-dungeon as of the end of Session 12**  
**Last Known Action:** **Sessions 11-12** - the party travelled out, were stopped and lied to by an OOTS patrol, were visited by Morvault overnight, then blew the reinforced front door off the chateau and caught **every goblin above ground in the surprise round**. Session 12 was a crawl through the ruins beneath: they cleared everything **except Room 4** (3 x Goblin Warlock, never entered), solved the stone-slab riddle cleanly with no arrow damage, and reached the boss room bloodied and battered to find **Vanya Windros caged, beaten, bleeding and crying**. **Lightbender Yeesal is still standing - the boss fight has not happened yet.** The party also confirmed the **Lumina Elixir operation never stopped** - Ars Lapis-bound barrels plus shipping manifests, marked for the Gem Families.  
**Next Trigger:** Session 13 opens on the Yeesal fight (3 x Goblin Boss + Yeesal) against an already-bloodied party  
**Notes:** Reframed from a "hunted" arc into a party-initiated revenge raid - Kaelen wants the nest that bred Hoogle the First's killer, not just the killer (already dead). Lower-stakes, hack-and-slash session by design. Goblins at the Lab greeted with "Hail the Order, the Order of the Sun" - possible DOL alignment is an open question for whether the aftermath draws institutional attention. **Reframed in play:** the raid went ahead as **debt leverage** (find the bank manager, use her against Morvault), *not* as Kaelen's Hoogle revenge arc - the revenge framing and its caged-Warg closure beat were dropped and are no longer tracked. The OOTS patrol lying to the party in Session 11 confirms institutional cover; whether that patrol reported upward (and so whether the Lorewarden knows the camp is being cleared) is still open. See [Goblin Settlement](../Rumours%20&%20Jobs/Solvetra/Rumours/Goblin%20Settlement.md).
