## Valerius' Replacement - Dorian Vasse
**Target:** Not the party specifically - see Notes  
**Threat:** Headguard Dorian Vasse - promoted from internal audit, not the streets. Vanilla DOL drone: procedural, rule-abiding, genuinely loyal to the DOL mission, no political cunning. Tightened Vault security: daily rotating countersign on B1/B2 sign-in, increased fixed-schedule Vault Room patrols, and a post-incident sign-in log review if the duress alarm ever trips.  
**Urgency:** High - timeline unknown, but the Lumina Lab's personnel were wiped out and the vault heist is imminent  
**Last Known Action:** Sessions 13-14 - the party missed the public announcement and heard about Vasse secondhand from NPCs and commoners (consensus: "a bit of a wet noodle"). Earlier: referenced in letter on Valerius' body: *"I've already organised your replacement."* Session 7: Tailendra's newspaper confirms a new Headguard arrives in 2 days - not yet chosen/announced publicly. Session 9: newspaper confirms the public reveal - announcement underneath the Solvetra Windmill (Middle Solvetra) at noon, 24 hours out.  
**Next Trigger:** Headguard revealed publicly at the Windmill announcement - DOL learns the vault has been robbed, or identifies the party by name  
**Notes:** Identity confirmed - Dorian Vasse. Reframed: the replacement is a vanilla DOL drone - a step up from Valerius in procedure and rigour, not in cunning or politics. He's not the mole's handler and has no awareness of intelligence assets (that runs through L.E. directly). The DOL already expected a Shadowbringer move (see The Lorewarden Emeritus, below) - this replacement's brief is to tighten security and counter/contain that anticipated operation (the vault) through stricter procedure, not to hunt the party by name. The party is at risk by *association* with the Shadowbringers, not yet personally. The DOL's overall posture is "contain quietly" - restaffing the Lab and slotting in this replacement are routine internal fixes, not a visible crackdown. Vasse's daily countersign is the specific procedural gotcha that threatens Kaelen's Valerius disguise. See [Lorewarden Emeritus](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Lorewarden%20Emeritus.md) and [Solvetra Headguard Replacement](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Dorian%20Vasse.md).

**Urgency:** Low-Medium - contract is live but Zatharial hasn't acted on it yet

(Note: Morvault made his personal appearance in Session 11; Zatharial making his while the Morvault clock is running would put both claims in the party's face at once.)  
**Next Trigger:** Zatharial appears and tears up the contract publicly, a power move dressed as a gesture of goodwill - carried forward, not yet played  
**Notes:** Runs in parallel with Lucien Morvault's claim - two live claims on the party simultaneously through Session 7. Zatharial believes the party are destined to defeat the Light Vassal. Operates independently of DOL and Shadowbringers. His Session 8 move will come with a smile and a proposition. His awareness of the party's involvement in the Lab is unconfirmed. Full mole detail (DM only) in his own file: [Lorewarden Emeritus](../../NPCs/Villains/Solvetra/Lorewarden%20Emeritus.md).
