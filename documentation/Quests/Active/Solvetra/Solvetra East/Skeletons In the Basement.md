## Session 9

Fight resolved - Fenrik and Halden fought off 5 x Skeletons and won; Halden paid the full 250gp to Fenrik.

Afterward, Fenrik investigated downstairs where a row of coffins lay and found a detailed mural of Tre Illium on the coffin room wall - Myxtiferious transitioning from dragon to dragonborn form, alongside Kaela Shadowsong and Sibling Thorne. Stylised, not literally recognisable as specific individuals. Small title on the mural: **"Luxfracta."**

One of the coffins held two giant angel feathers - unknowingly Sibling Thorne's; he made his way out of one of these coffins at some point (DM note: reason TBD, not known to the party). Halden told Fenrik those interred here are called the **Lunarbringers** - see [Tre Illium](../../../../History%20&%20Lore/Tre%20Illium.md). Halden has more to say on this - follow up next session.

The party (Fenrik, Frah'nk, Elaris) returned the next day to investigate further - no new information surfaced, but Elaris's necklace reacted strongly to the residual energy in the room.
