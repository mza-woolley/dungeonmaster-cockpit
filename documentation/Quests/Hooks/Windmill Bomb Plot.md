# IDEA: Windmill Bomb Plot

**Concept:** The "clock" Wizzleforth defused in Middle Solvetra wasn't just a stalled construction project - it was the cover for a terrorist plot. Middle Solvetra was building a giant clock intended for the Solvetra Windmill (Moonglass Mill, "Sun's Shine" - Miller Gavric Lorne, Farmhand Ettie Rowen), but someone planted a bomb inside the clock mechanism itself, which is why progress on it stalled. Wizzleforth defused it and is now the only person who knows what it really was. He's sitting on knowledge of an attempted attack on the Windmill, and no one else in the party - or in Middle Solvetra - knows yet.

---

## What We Know So Far

- The clock was meant for the Solvetra Windmill in Middle Solvetra.
- Construction/installation stalled - publicly unexplained, but the real reason is the bomb hidden inside it.
- Wizzleforth found and defused the bomb (referenced in Session 7 as "Middle Solv clock timebomb - defused, Wizzleforth has").
- He has told no one else in the party what he actually found.
- Frah'nk enquired about the stalled clock thread during Session 7 and rolled a dirty 20 - worth remembering if he circles back and pushes on it again.

---

## Open Questions

- Who planted the bomb, and why the Windmill specifically? DOL sabotage, Shadowbringers overreach, NSPF, or a third party not yet introduced?
- Was the target the Windmill itself (the mill, the grain supply, the district's economy) or something/someone expected to be near it when the clock was unveiled?
- Does Wizzleforth ever bring this to the rest of the party, or does he sit on it the way Adrion sat on his contract - another crack under pressure?
- Is this connected to anything else already in motion (the DOL's finances post-heist, the Shadowbringers' wider campaign, or something entirely separate)?
- What does Wizzleforth actually have in hand now - a disarmed device, a fuse, a maker's mark - something that could be traced?
