# Lucien Morvault

**Type:** Creditor / Antagonist  
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

A creditor with clear magical capability. Sent a letter to Adrion in Solvetra Intermellum demanding repayment of **5,000 gold pieces** — framed as a previously agreed debt. His tone is cold and precise, with no room for negotiation implied.

## Known Capabilities

- Can cast **Mass Polymorph** — threatened to transform Adrion and his companions into insects if the debt goes unpaid.

## Known Letter (Session 7)

> "The time has come — I require the payment from you. 5,000 gold pieces as we agreed upon. I'll meet you in Solvetra Intermellum in 5 night's time. If I don't see you, I will teleport to you and Mass Polymorph you and any friends of yours into pathetic little bugs I can squash beneath my boot. Your choice, Adrion."
> — *Lucien Morvault*

## Notes

- Silas confirmed there is no realistic way the party has this kind of gold through normal means, and only one viable path to acquire it — the Solvetra Vault heist.
- His connection to Adrion's backstory (the nature of the "agreement") is currently unexplored.
