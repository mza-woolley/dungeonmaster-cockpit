# Måneklang

**Type:** Magic Item — Instrument / Weapon  
**Holder:** Kaelen  
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

A magical instrument wielded by Kaelen. It resonates with a dangerous harmonic hum capable of lethal sonic output. Its name and origin are not yet documented in the world's records.

**Found:** Solvetra Lighthouse, Session 2 — see [Session 2 & 3](../../Sessions/Notes/Session%202%20%26%203%20-%20Solvetra%20Meadows.md).

---

## Abilities

**Måneklang's Hum**  
Once per short rest, at the start of the first battle — when Kaelen grants Bardic Inspiration to an ally, the Hum activates. The target *closest* to the inspired ally must roll a **DC8 Wisdom saving throw** or their head explodes from the resonance.

- Used at **The False Stage** (Solvetra Middle) to evaporate the performing bards with a burst of sound
