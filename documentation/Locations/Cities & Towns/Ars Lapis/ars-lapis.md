# Ars Lapis

A fortress-city built to guard the region's most coveted resource: its deep-veined mines of precious gems. The city's name - Lapis, meaning "stone" - is both literal and symbolic. Everything in Ars Lapis is carved from permanence: its towering walls, its rigid hierarchy, and its unyielding devotion to extraction.

Governed by a monopolistic autocracy, power is concentrated among a few elite mining houses sanctioned by the Division of Lumination. These families control not only the gem trade, but the lives of the citizens who toil beneath the earth. Most inhabitants are miners, labourers, or support workers, bound by generational contracts and the weight of tradition.

Despite its harsh structure, Ars Lapis is revered for its fortified beauty. Its castle, with many towers, stands as both a symbol of protection and control. Trade is tightly regulated and outsiders are viewed with suspicion unless bearing sanctioned contracts or rare offerings.
