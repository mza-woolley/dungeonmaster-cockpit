# Goblin Settlement

**Location:** Unknown — Solvetra outskirts (unconfirmed)  
**Status:** Active — unvisited. Revenge arc pending.

---

## Overview

A goblin settlement whose kin were employed (and effectively enslaved) as labour and enforcers within the Lumina Lab. The party cleared the Lab entirely — killing Boss Dragg, the Goblin Boss, and all Goblin Warriors present.

The interrogated Goblin Warrior had no stated camp association, suggesting the goblins' primary loyalty was to the Lab's leadership rather than a home settlement. However, a settlement almost certainly exists — and word of what happened will reach it.

The party killed their boss, their warriors, and dismantled their operation. The settlement will want blood.
