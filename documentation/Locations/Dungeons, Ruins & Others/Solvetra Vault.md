# Solvetra Vault

**Location:** Solvetra Middle  
**Status:** Active - heist in planning (Session 7)

---

## Overview

The primary financial institution of Solvetra, operated under Division of Lumination control. Citizens' funds have been effectively seized by the DOL for their cause. The vault itself is blast-resistant with a 4-lock simultaneous-entry system, and is guarded on the lower floors by **Order of the Sun (OOTS)** operatives.

**Session 8:** Confirmed via Gruunsh'ck Diamondholder that the Vault currently isn't letting citizens withdraw their money - consistent with the funds-seizure lore above, but now explicitly stated in-scene rather than only inferred from Investigation checks.

The structure is built into a cave system, with a river running beneath the lower floors.

Refer to map for layout detail.

> *L1, B1, and the Vault Room below are standing facts about those spaces - true whenever the party arrives, via whatever entry route (front door, sewer, chimney), in whatever order. Nothing assumes they've done the others first.*

## L1 - Public Floor

PCs walk into an apparently empty bank. Investigation/Insight:
- **Nat1:** Ripe for a steal right now - guards distracted.
- **DC2–13:** Quiet, no customers, but guards and tellers still working.
- **DC13–17:** Guards have unguarded chests of gold behind the desk; there's an area behind the teller wall.
- **DC17–20:** Probably empty because the Lumination has taken control of everyone's funds and no one seems to care.
- **DC20–25:** 6 chests visible, ~175gp max each - a strangely specific amount.
- **DC25+/Nat20:** That gold is "petty cash" for citizen transactions - the real stash is deeper in the bank.

**Getting downstairs** needs a Working Bank Rights Card and a Valid ID. PCs can learn this from the three (elf) tellers, or get turned away by guards if they try the right-side passage.

| Teller | Personality | Pipeline |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Traelensch Barthum (left) | Eloquent older gentleman, kind to requests, claims to be new and keeps fetching his supervisor (Kaeren). Shadowbringer inside contact - see [Active Quests](../../Quests/Active/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/The%20Big%20One.md). | - |
| Kaeren Arguback (middle) | Feisty, demands a Form 32B for the Card + ID (DC25+/Nat20 on her speech checks). Asks for the PC's mother's maiden name and closest living relative. | Helps fill out Form 64GB |
| Gruunsh'ck Diamondholder (right) | Nerdy goblin, "akshually"s the party through the bureaucracy. | Form 1A (chosen class) → Form 1475L (goals/ambitions) → Form 69X (sexual activity in last 45 days) → Form 32B (what would you do if someone tried to steal all the gold? - acceptable answers range from immediate surrender to hitting the red button under every desk) |

**The guards** (8 visible, all sound like Shax from Destiny 2). Investigation/Insight if questioned:
- **DC1–14:** Here to stop robberies and kill any wrongdoers.
- **DC14–19:** Not in the murder business - trained to detain and knock out suspects.
- **DC19+/Nat20:** Mostly just stand around; lately citizens have seemed a bit more chirpy.
- They look strong - probably best not to provoke them.

**Teller desks:** DC1–13 nothing of note / DC13+ a pamphlet reminding guards not to leave their post without first alerting tellers with "Visitors to Basement Floor, stand aside!" - otherwise tellers may interrogate visitors and hit the lockdown button.

## B1 - Basement Floor

PCs descend into the basement. Near the wall, group Nature check on water flowing beyond it:
- **DC1–21:** Might've heard something, probably imagining it.
- **DC21+/Nat20:** That's definitely flowing water.

Around the wall: a large space with 8 worker desks. Group Investigation/Insight:
- **Nat1:** These people are clearly prepping a heist - just ask them where the vault is.
- **DC1–10:** Normal work chatter over documents.
- **DC10–14:** Each desk's transaction history tops out around 250gp.
- **DC15–20:** 8 desks, 8 workers - the bank's still running at full force despite no customers upstairs. Likely tied to Vanya's office.
- **DC21+:** Lit fireplaces + a sewer grate with audible running water - is this place built into a cave?
- **DC Nat20:** Not cold, but a slight chill/breeze near the fireplaces - a draft from somewhere.

**Desk NPCs:**
- **Bottom-left - Selene Faerwyn & Tavian Grellan:** No info on the vault or keys, but praise Vanya as a manager who always protected her staff; now they just process petty payments/withdrawals.
- **Middle-top - Joss Caelthorn (team leader) & Mirelle Windmere:** Anxious about the missing keys. Joss holds a spare key (same design as Vanya's), doesn't know what it opens - Vanya asked him to keep it as a "safe spare." Mirelle's never seen it and thinks he should hide it. PCs can only get the key from Joss by appealing to authority and presenting themselves credibly (Insight DC15).

**Sewer grates** confirm a nearby river. Nature check (advantage if rolled earlier):
- **DC1–10:** Just flowing water they couldn't build over.
- **DC11–17:** If built into a cave/alcove, the sewer probably drains into the town river.
- **DC18+:** This river is likely part of the same cave system as further bank extensions.
- **DC Nat20:** Can hear/feel the water heading toward the wall - toward the vault.

**Vanya's Room:** visible from outside, locked (DC17 simple lock - pick DC17, or smash with STR DC12+ and Stealth DC17+ to avoid detection). A small viewing window shows a golden key on the desk. Once inside, Insight:
- **DC1–13:** The key's there for taking, no magic detected. There's an unlit fireplace.
- **DC14–18:** Oddly clean for someone who vanished - fresh fruit and baskets on the table.
- **DC19–24:** The fireplace is spotless, no soot in the chimney. A drawer note reads: *"Arcane Vault Guards are to follow arcanum protocol for all of those who wish to enter or view the vault. No exceptions."*
- **DC25+/Nat20:** Slight draft from the chimney, dirt footprints at its base. An attached note lists: Message, Invisibility, Disguise Self, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, Detect Object.

## Vault Room

Entry via the Sewer/River Grate or the Chimney. PCs roll Arcana + CHA save (DC12+) on entry. Describe the room and the vault door's 4 key slots.

- **Sewer entry:** DC18+ Speech to convince the OOTS guards they're supposed to be there once they surface - guards drop a hook and ask about scheduled river maintenance (it smells like there's a body down there). Advantage on this roll *only if* they've dealt with the monster in the pipes. Once convinced, guards treat them as friendly maintenance workers.
- **Chimney entry** (the assumed/intended route): guards eyeball the party as they arrive but don't react further - three guards, a desk with a money counter, and the vault door.

**Perception on the OOTS guards:**
- **DC13+:** They treat everyone equally regardless of rank, and follow a strict process every time.
- **DC20+:** Their armour bears a Sun symbol - the Order of the Sun?

**Identity verification** (dynamic - varies by approach):
- **Intern approach:** Arcana check only.
- **Loud approach:** Guards fight.
- **Sneak approach:** Unseen unless something breaks cover, which turns it into the Loud approach.
- **Sewer approach:** Arcana + Investigation from both door guards, with disadvantage on Investigation. The table guard readies their key - roll Perception at that moment, as PCs may spot it and snag another key.

3 × OOTS Guard (Spy statblock).

## Security System

Every teller/worker desk has a panic button underneath, meant to summon guards in an emergency - never been pressed, as far as anyone knows. To learn more, PCs need to examine a button up close inside the bank. Arcana check:
- **DC1–12:** Definitely magic, but unclear what it'd actually do.
- **DC13–20:** Almost certainly alerts guards to converge on the location.
- **DC21+/Nat20:** No one nearby is equipped to *receive* that signal - there must be guards elsewhere, not yet found, who'd respond.

## Vault Door & Keys

4 key slots, blast-resistant, all 4 must be filled simultaneously to open normally.

| Key | Location |
|-----|----------|
| Key 1 | Vanya's desk (B1 office) |
| Key 2 | Traelensch Barthum (bank teller, inside contact) - hands it over if asked/pressured |
| Key 3 | Table guard in Vault Room |
| Key 4 | Lorewarden Emeritus - not reachable via any of the above leads |

The 4th key is a deliberate surprise - at the planning stage, the party and Silas's crew only know about 3 locks/keys (see [Session 7](../../Sessions/Notes/Session%207%20-%20The%20Big%20One.md)). If the party reaches the door with fewer than 4 keys, it simply doesn't open the conventional way. That's not a fail state - it's the actual shape of the problem. Whatever they do next (force it, find another route, walk away, come back later) follows from whatever they've got and try in the moment.

## GM Reference - Spell Scenarios

- **Message:** ends once its task is achieved - an NPC may complete the task and snap out of the Charm mid-conversation. Bank tellers are trained against Speech/Charm (high DCs, +3 WIS saves). Otherwise, allow it freely barring extreme circumstances.
- **Invisibility:** regular tellers/guards can't detect it at all. Only the OOTS (Dawnbringer) guards downstairs can Detect Magic - they roll Arcana only when interacting with a *visible* PC, sensing the invisible one via osmosis, never randomly. Give a vague heads-up that it's finicky so PCs keep an action ready (DEX save vs. a guard throwing a sword at the Arcana-detected PC).
- **Disguise Self:** a guard studying the PC rolls Investigation vs. Spell Save DC - no adv/disadv unless the PC does something thematically bold.
- **Minor Illusion / Prestidigitation:** same Investigation vs. Spell Save DC check, but the studying guard sends *another* guard to investigate - effectively pulling one guard out of the encounter.
- **Detect Object:** blocked by thick lead walls except through the vault. Arcana check: Nat1 no gold at all / DC1–7 something's blocking magic - a lead wall / DC7+ blocked, but a "metric fuckton" of gold is sensed behind the lead wall (or the vault door).
