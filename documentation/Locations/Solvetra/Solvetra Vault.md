# Solvetra Vault

**Location:** Solvetra Middle  
**Status:** Active — heist in planning (Session 7)

---

## Overview

The primary financial institution of Solvetra, operated under Division of Lumination control. Citizens' funds have been effectively seized by the DOL for their cause. The vault itself is blast-resistant with a 4-lock simultaneous-entry system, and is guarded on the lower floors by **Order of the Sun (OOTS)** operatives.

The structure is built into a cave system, with a river running beneath the lower floors.

Refer to map for layout detail.

---

## Structure

**L1 — Public Floor**  
Main banking hall. Three tellers, 8 visible guards. Gold chests kept in the open are petty cash only. Access to lower floors requires a Working Bank Rights Card and Valid ID.

**B1 — Basement Floor**  
Eight bank worker desks. Vanya Windros' office (locked). Sewer grates over the river below. OOTS guards stationed here.

**Vault Room**  
Accessible via chimney (intended) or sewer/river grate. Three OOTS guards. A 4-key vault door — 4 locks, must be opened simultaneously. The vault door is blast-resistant.
