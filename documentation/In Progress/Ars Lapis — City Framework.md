# Ars Lapis — City Framework
_Answer these questions to build the city. Delete the prompts as you go._

---

## Identity — What kind of city is this?

- In one sentence, what is Ars Lapis *for*? (Trade hub? Administrative seat? Military stronghold? Cultural capital?)
- What does it look like at a glance — architecture, scale, mood? How does it contrast with Solvetra?
- What's the one thing every traveller knows about Ars Lapis before they arrive?
- Is this a city people *want* to visit, or one they end up in?

---

## The Division of Lumination Here

- How far along is the DOL's grip on Ars Lapis compared to Solvetra? (Just arriving / well established / fully converted?)
- Is their presence subtle (bureaucratic, economic) or visible (guards, flags, public ceremonies)?
- The Lumina Elixir distribution map pointed here — has it arrived yet? Is it being tested? Has it already worked?
- Who is the DOL's face in Ars Lapis — a Headguard like Valerius, a priest, a political appointee?
- Is there a local institution the DOL has co-opted (a guild, a bank, a temple)? Or did they build their own?

---

## Resistance & Tension

- Is there an active Shadowbringer cell here, or is Silas building one from scratch?
- Who in this city *knows* something is wrong but hasn't acted — and why not?
- Is there a faction here that isn't DOL or Shadowbringer — a neutral power, a criminal outfit, a merchant bloc — who has their own agenda?
- What's the pressure point? What one thing, if it broke, would change everything in this city?

---

## What Silas Wants Here

- What is Silas actually planning in Ars Lapis? (Retrieval? Assassination? Alliance? Sabotage?)
- Why does it require the party specifically — what can't the Shadowbringers do alone?
- What's the risk Silas isn't telling them about?
- What does success in Ars Lapis mean for the broader war against the DOL?
