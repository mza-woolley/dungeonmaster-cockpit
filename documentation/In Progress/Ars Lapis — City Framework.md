# Ars Lapis — City Framework

---

## Identity — What kind of city is this?

The city of Ars Lapis is one of mining and fine physically good ownership and trading. Where the grit of trade is happening, is likely where Ars Lapis is. Ars Lapis is primarly known for their gem trade. These gems are attuned to light magic and are able to both provide and suck the light out of space, acting as a form of power source for the nation.

It's a thriving community circulated around the trade of "Lumina Gems" and their usage. As mentioned, there is also trade of fine/rare goods, so naturally there are many wealthy people here - but there is also quite an urban sprawl of those trying to cash in on this circular-economy.

Architecture is fine, but a little comtemporary as the town's basis is off the back of the demand of Lumina Gems. The scale, it's the 2nd largest pop. count in Le'Choix, but probably takes up the most space due to the mining operations. The vibe is that of New York mixed with a "hustler's vibe"; everyone is trying to be succesful and make it big, especially those in the urban circle and the outskirts.

Solvetra is a place where people live; Ars Lapis is a place where people succeed - and usually, at any cost. It's not a theives's den, but it's like the top floor of a Stock Exchange where the ethically questionable handshakes happen.

Everyone knows about Ars Lapis and it's 3 "Gem Families" as they call it; the families who have profited off the Gem Trade generationally and contributed to the soaring growth of the area. More in "The Gem Families" (Factions). They obviously too know about the Lumina Gem harvesting and the ethical issues with how miners are treated down there, in Echo Vains.

It's particular a city where people want to be in, but it's one they know they have to be in if they have a commodity or an asset worth selling. If you're looking for the casual, slow-paced lifetyle - Ars Lapis is the wrong place. Everyone is looking to sell, and investors are looking to profit, profit, profit!

People who end up here have nothing to offer unless by intention, and contribute to nothing Ars Lapis-wide. This strip of town is called "Bankrupt Promenade".

## The Division of Lumination Here

The DOL influence isn't particular malicious by design, they just have a goal and are attempting to unify the nation in their ways - which is at worse, a little unethical. However - their dealings and support of the "Gem Families" is a hidden and atriocious relationship where the DOL turn a blind eye to their mining activites in the pursuit of pure profit. The DOL are fully interwoven into this aspect of La'Choix.

Their presence is completely invisible - it's more of a symbiotic relationship with the DOL. The Gem Families wouldn't really be able to have the market/standardisation throught the world for their Lumina Gems without the DOL, but if the Gem Families weren't on the DOL's side - they would not be able to establish any influence and would crumble to the Gem Families guerrilla tactics.

There are of course guards as there would be normally/usually. There are is of course as well the "mafia" per family which take care of business as required. The DOL absolutely do not get in the way of this; it's a loss-loss, no-point situation.

Lumina Elixir arrived quite a while ago, and has been used/abused (see Lumina Elixir, Magic Items). This phenomena drives the line between prosperity and complete failure/loss.

## Resistance & Tension

There is no Shadowbringer activity in Ars Lapis, but Silas knows about the Stock Exchange and wants to concoct a plan to funnel funds away from the DOL and cause chaos for investors, at the consequence of the DOL.

People in the city/out of the city kind-of know that off the back of the mining trade - that miners are treated poorly and are effectively being abused (borderline). People have not acted on this as they see miners as "9-5ers" due to the hustle culture of the city. This causes people not to have empathy because everyone is a hunter; not a gatherer.

There is criminal activity and 3 main criminal outfits - they are the Gem Families. They have an agenda of skimming some off the top, but to also invest in solid products/opportunties to continue to cement their multi-generational legacy.

- What's the pressure point? What one thing, if it broke, would change everything in this city?

The pressure point in Ars Lapis is definitely if the miner's unionised/striked - the production of Lumina Gems would cease, ensuring local vendors lose their buying power which slowly halts the cyclical economy of Ars Lapis - which is bad news bears for the nation. A better approach to this (TBC). It's not really a fragile economy, but the money is funneled toward 2 competing, power-hungry entities.
