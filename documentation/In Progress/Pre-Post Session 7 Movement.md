## Suggestions

- **Key 4 is the session's hidden pivot.** The Lorewarden holds it. The party likely won't get it cleanly. Design the vault door moment so 3-key entry is possible but costly - the 4th lock as a live problem is more interesting than a solved one.
- **Traelensch is a pressure cooker.** He's already done "one last job." If the party leans on him too hard, he cracks under Kaeren's watch. Let the players feel that instability - don't resolve it for them.
- **Zatharial should appear if things go badly for Adrion.** The Consequence Framework already flags this. Have him ready: composed, smiling, one proposition that sounds like a favour.
- **Kaelen's Valerius disguise has a shelf life.** Valerius is dead and a replacement is already organised. If Kaelen uses the face at the vault, the clock is ticking - the DOL will notice inconsistency eventually. Use that as a slow-burn complication into Ars Lapis.
- **Let the goblin settlement rumour surface on the road.** They don't need to appear yet - just a traveller who heard something. Plants the arc without triggering it.
- **Wizzleforth and the vault records** - if there's anything in the vault that could crack his lineage open (a name, a document, a Glimmerdeep reference), plant it. He's been reading DOL texts for two sessions. He's ready.
- **The Echo Unheard still hasn't followed up.** If there's a quiet moment post-heist, a small resonance - Måneklang humming unprompted, a name someone says - keeps the thread alive without forcing it.
