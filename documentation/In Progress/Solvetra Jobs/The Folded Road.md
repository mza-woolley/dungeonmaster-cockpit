# The Folded Road (Inn)
**District:** Solvetra East

## Brail Mothen — Innkeeper
Keeps a no-frills inn for people passing through rather than staying — practical, unsentimental.

## Coris Jent — Civilian (Traveler)
One of the inn's passers-through, restless and eager to talk about wherever they've just come from. A ready-made rumour-delivery NPC.
