# Streets of Solvetra Intermellum
**District:** Solvetra Intermellum

## Tailendra — Town Crier / Newspaper Vendor (Human)
Loud, fast-talking, always working the crowd — half the district's gossip and half its actual news comes filtered through Tailendra one way or another.

**Jobs:**
- **Scoop of the Week** — Tailendra's heard a rumor too good to print without confirmation (something DOL-adjacent, or tied to an active party thread) and needs it verified before a rival crier beats them to it. Pays for the confirmed story, more for an exclusive angle.
- **Drown It Out** — Someone's been paying a rival vendor to spread an unflattering story about Tailendra (or about a client of theirs). They want the party to find out who's bankrolling it and either scare them off or dig up dirt to run a counter-story.
