# Middle Solvetra Lightstation
**District:** Solvetra Middle

## Irene Valcour — Keeper
Brisk and procedural, the administrative backbone of the Lightstation.

**Jobs:**
- **Missing Paperwork** — A shipment manifest that should've cleared Irene's desk weeks ago never arrived, and she suspects it's tangled up with a bribed clerk further down the chain. She wants discreet outsiders to trace it without triggering an internal DOL inquiry that could implicate her office.
- **Keep the Peace** — Two rival tradesguilds are due to renew a permit at the Lightstation on the same day, and Irene knows their reps will come to blows in her lobby if left alone. She'll pay for chaperones to keep both parties civil until the paperwork's signed.

## Brother Calis — Sun Envoy
The DOL's softer public face — warm voice, scripted reassurances, sent out specifically to make the Order look approachable.

**Jobs:**
- **Good Press** — Calis needs a genuinely good deed done in his name to offset some bad DOL press circulating in Solvetra Middle — feeding a hungry block, rescuing something from a fire, anything sincere he can publicly credit to "the Order's outreach."
- **Off Script** — Calis privately doubts a talking point he's been told to recite about a recent DOL action and wants outsiders to quietly find out if it's true before he repeats it to a crowd again — he can't be seen investigating his own superiors.
