# Solvetra East Lightstation
**District:** Solvetra East

## Yann Corren — Keeper
Rigid and procedural, runs the East Lightstation with textbook discipline.

**Jobs:**
- **Audit Escort** — Yann's quarterly supply audit is due and the courier who usually runs the ledgers to Solvetra Middle went missing two days ago. He needs someone "procedurally sound" to escort the paperwork (and quietly ask around about the courier) without drawing DOL attention.
- **By the Book** — A minor infraction (an unlicensed street vendor, an unpermitted bonfire) has Yann stuck: his own rules require a written complaint before he can act, but nobody local will put their name on one out of fear of reprisal. He'll pay for someone unaffiliated to file it and "witness" the resulting citation.

## Telra Wens — Watcher
Sharp-eyed and suspicious of strangers in a district that doesn't get many visitors.

**Jobs:**
- **New Faces** — Telra's noticed the same unfamiliar cloaked figure pass through the checkpoint three times this week, always alone, always at dusk. She can't leave her post to follow — she wants outsiders (who won't spook the target) to tail them and report back.
- **Prove Me Wrong** — Telra flagged a "traveling merchant" as suspicious weeks ago and was overruled by Yann. If the party can quietly investigate the merchant's stall and dig up anything incriminating (smuggling, forged papers, whatever), she'll pay out of her own pocket to be vindicated.
