# Lightstation Solvetra West
**District:** Solvetra West

## Fletcher Brok — DOL Guard
Stationed alongside Tate Wexley and Headguard Valerius du Noirveil — by-the-book and unremarkable, escalates to his Headguard rather than making a call himself.

**Jobs:**
- **Above My Pay Grade** — Fletcher found something during a routine patrol (contraband, a body, a strange sigil) that he's too nervous to report up the chain without more information first. He'll quietly pay outsiders to look into it so he can bring his Headguard a fuller picture instead of a half-formed guess.
- **Cover the Shift** — Fletcher needs to be somewhere off the books for a few hours (family matter, a debt, a tail he wants to lose) and needs the party to create a distraction or plausible reason for his absence at the Lightstation without it reaching Valerius.

## Tate Wexley — DOL Guard
Stationed alongside Fletcher Brok and Headguard Valerius du Noirveil — background muscle for the Lightstation.

**Jobs:**
- **Muscle for Hire** — Off-duty, Tate moonlights as quiet backup for locals who can't afford real protection. He's overcommitted himself this week and wants to sub the job out to capable outsiders — a merchant needs a delivery escorted, or a debtor needs "gently" reminded.
- **Bad Bet** — Tate owes money to the wrong crowd from an underground fighting ring. He can't be seen paying it off directly without questions from the Lightstation, so he wants the party to deliver the payment (or work off the debt some other way) quietly.
