# The Final Rest (Inn)
**District:** Solvetra Middle

## Taron Velle — Innkeeper
Gruff but reliable, keeps "the really good, dark ale" behind the bar for the right people — the go-between for Silas Shadowfoot's meeting with the party.

## Myra Dens — Housemaid
Quick and quiet, more aware of who's-meeting-who in the common room than she lets on.
