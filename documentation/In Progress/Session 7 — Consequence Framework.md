# Session 7 — Consequence Framework
_The vault heist is the pivot point of the first arc. Everything before it built to this. Everything after it flows from it. Answer these questions before you run it._

---

## What's Actually At Stake

Before mapping outcomes, be clear on the layers:

- **Adrion's debt** — 5,000gp to Lucien Morvault in 5 nights. The 5,000gp is a buyout clause — the original bargain was Adrion handing over the next adventuring party (the PCs) to Morvault in exchange for his own escape after his village burned. Failure to pay defaults to that original term: Lucien collects the party itself via a binding claim, not combat. See [Lucien Morvault](../NPCs/Villains/Lucien%20Morvault.md).
- **Draining the DOL's funds** — the vault holds DOL funds. The Shadowbringers don't care where the gold ends up afterward — what matters is that it's out of DOL hands. Draining it cripples the Division's finances in Solvetra.
- **The DOL's symbolic power** — the vault *is* the Division's control over citizens. Robbing it is a public act of defiance whether the party wants it to be or not.
- **The party's cover** — after the Lumina Lab, the DOL already know these people exist. The vault heist ends that ambiguity entirely.

## The Four Outcomes

### A — Full Success
The party gets the gold, pays Lucien, the vault is drained from DOL control, nobody dies, nobody is identified by name (though the DOL knows it was a heist — they just don't know who did it).

- Does the DOL know it was robbed immediately, or is there a window of confusion?

The DOL may know about the bank heist, but what determines what/if the DOL knows who has done it. At the very least, the DOL should know that there was a thread or two that ties the operation to those in Solvetra Intermellum. I think the vibe here is there the DOL has become complacent in years on their authorative measure and therefore would let crime talks, even from the crime-capital Solvera Intermellum, fall through the cracks. It's dependant on whether the player's too aren't raising suspicion within Solvetra when they are talking to NPCs to acquire items/goods from the Heist.

- How does the DOL explain the missing funds to the citizens whose money it was?

The DOL will spin this positively - they will be able to replace the funds 1:1 due to their underworld operations, so this is just their legalfront. As they replace this money, they will use this as a PR activity in order to get the trust of it's citizens - especially if there are people/citizens who witness the crime happening. Citizens would react with gratefulness, shifting the responsibility of the issue with the bank from the DOL to unidentified thieves. The DOL knows it was a heist - they just don't know who's responsible.

### B — Partial Success
They get some gold — enough for Lucien, not enough to cripple the DOL, or vice versa. Or they get in and out but leave a trail.

- If they can only do one — pay Lucien or fund the Shadowbringers — which do they choose, and what does the unchosen consequence look like?

If/once the PCs steal the money - they are 100% are doing whatever they feel is necessary with the money. If they pay Lucien, if they don't, etc. They will not have to pay or fund the Shadowbringer however as the point of the heist isn't for the Shadowbringers to revenue raise; it's for the Shadowbringers to bring a financial crippling to the DOL, so that they may thin resources and tear down their walls, slowly.

- If they leave a trail (a witness, a dead guard, Traelensch rats them out), how quickly does the DOL identify the party by name?

The DOL shouldn't really be able to finger the PCs as the problem. The thing being that, the DOL only really knows about the Shadowbringers as there more "active" rebellious group in Solvetra, but they've always been kind of passive (DOL interpretation). In reality, the Shadowbringers have fingers in many pies and are actually quite active on the anti-establishment of the DOL. In this, the DOL would only think the Shadowbringers can be behind this, and not the newly arrived PCs - and even then, they would know about it as they underestimate the Shadowbringers.

### C — Heist Fails, Party Escapes
They don't get the gold but get out alive. Now they have the debt and no plan.

- What does Lucien Morvault actually do when the deadline passes — does he follow through on Mass Polymorph, or does he want something else from Adrion?
- Does Zatharial see an opportunity here? Adrion's life is in danger — the contract becomes relevant. When and how does Zatharial resurface?
- Does Silas cut the party loose, or does he double down because they're still the best asset he has?
- Is there an alternative path to 5,000gp that opens up — a job, a mark, an NPC who'd pay for something the party could do?

---

### D — Catastrophic Failure
Captured, killed, or publicly exposed. The DOL now has the party by name, face, and affiliation.

- If the party is captured — what does the DOL do with them? Execute them publicly (too messy)? Interrogate them (what do they know)? Use them as leverage against the Shadowbringers?
- If they escape but are publicly named as enemies of the Order — what changes in Solvetra? Can they walk the streets? Does anyone shelter them?
- Does Silas sacrifice the party to protect the broader rebellion, or does he burn resources to get them out?
- What does this do to the Shadowbringers' position in Solvetra — are they exposed too, or does the DOL not know the connection yet?

## Villain Threads — Where Are They After This?

### The DOL & The Lorewarden Emeritus
- Valerius is dead. His replacement was "already organised." Does the heist accelerate that replacement's arrival?
- L.E. signed the Lumina Elixir distribution map. He holds the 4th vault key. Has he been in Solvetra this whole time, watching? Or is this the event that brings him here personally?
- What does the Lorewarden *actually want* — permanent rule through light, yes, but what's the personal motivation underneath that? Power? Fear? Belief?
- Is there a moment where the party could meet him before a confrontation — and what would he say to them?

### Lucien Morvault
- The original agreement: Adrion's village burned, and to escape with his life he agreed to deliver the next adventuring party he fell in with — the PCs — to Morvault. The 5,000gp letter is a buyout clause, not the real debt.
- Run this as a parley, not a fight — Lucien is protected by contract-magic; attacking him during the meeting auto-defaults the bargain in his favour regardless of payment.
- Is Lucien connected to the DOL, the Shadowbringers, or neither?
- If paid — does he stay in play, or disappear? If he stays, what does he want next?
- If not paid — what does "collecting on the party" actually look like? A binding claim, a debt that follows them, a future call-in? Avoid combat resolution.
- Does this reveal land mid-heist (raising the stakes of failure) or only at the meeting itself?

### Zatharial
- He signed Adrion up to give away the party's souls on Adrion's death — that contract is **still active** through Session 7. The party's souls are currently bound, alongside Morvault's claim — two live claims at once.
- Zatharial's appearance and tear-up of the contract is **planned for Session 8**, not this session. He has a vested interest in the party defeating the Light Vassal — so he doesn't *want* them dead, but he hasn't shown his hand yet.
- Does the rest of the party know about the contract? If not — does Session 7 plant any hint that something is still hanging over them, ahead of the Session 8 reveal?
- Reserve the "split a wedge down the middle" angle for Session 8 — what's the wedge, which two party members are most at odds, and how does Zatharial exploit that once he appears?

### The Northern Star of the Primordial Fist
- They sent goons to Solvetra Meadows to kill Fenrik. Those goons are dead.
- Aurevon Beitomy Erstua ordered the hit. Does Aurevon escalate — send someone better, come personally, or try a different approach entirely?
- The party is now far louder than they were at the Meadows. Does the NSPF's increased profile on the party make them *more* aggressive or *more* cautious?
- Is there a moment during or after the heist where Fenrik realises the NSPF has found them again?

### The Goblin Settlement
- The party killed Boss Dragg and every goblin in the Lumina Lab. Word reaches the settlement.
- Does the revenge arc activate *during* the heist (worst possible timing), *after* it (when the party is already exposed), or is it a slow burn into Ars Lapis?
- What does the settlement want — blood, or something more specific (a body returned, a debt owed, an object taken)?

## Party-Internal Threads

These are the cracks. The heist pressure-tests all of them.

### Adrion's Secrets
- Adrion signed Zatharial's contract without reading it. He has a 5,000gp debt from an agreement no one else knows the origin of. His village burned down and he was confronted about it in Session 5.
- How much does the party actually know about Adrion's past at this point?
- Is this the session where something comes out — the contract, the debt's origin, the village — or does it stay buried one more arc?
- If the party learns about the soul contract mid-heist, what happens to the plan?

### Kaelen Has Valerius' Form
- Kaelen left Session 6 with the ability to disguise as Valerius du Noirveil.
- Valerius is dead — but no one outside the party knows that yet (presumably).
- How far can this be pushed? Does "Valerius" still have authority in the vault, or has his disappearance already been noted?
- What happens when Valerius' replacement arrives and the party is still using the face?

### Plumbodian's Lorewarden Quest
- The Lorewarden gave Plumbodian a mission back in Session 5. Plumbodian was reminded of it by note in Session 6.
- What *is* that quest — and is it in conflict with robbing the vault the Lorewarden effectively controls?
- Does completing the quest require Plumbodian to betray the party, or just keep a secret?
- The Lorewarden holds the 4th vault key. Does Plumbodian know this?

### Wizzleforth's Growing Understanding
- He's been reading DOL texts, gained Religion proficiency, and experienced what sounds like an astral projection of his own lineage in Session 5.
- Does he know yet that Elaris exists? That Virelle sank ships to protect herself from being found?
- Is there something in the vault — a record, a name, a document — that could crack his understanding of his own family open?

## Ripple Effects on the World

### If the DOL Loses the Vault
- Citizens' money was seized for DOL use. If the vault is robbed, does any of that money make it back to citizens? Does the Shadowbringer plan include redistribution, or is it purely tactical?
- How does the DOL fund operations in Solvetra without the vault? Do they pull resources from Ars Lapis? Does that weaken them elsewhere?
- Does losing the vault *publicly* — even if the DOL tries to suppress it — inspire other Shadowbringer cells to act?

### The Lumina Elixir
- Production in Solvetra is destroyed. The distribution map pointed to Ars Lapis.
- Is there a secondary production facility? Is Ars Lapis already receiving elixir from somewhere else?
- At some point the world finds out what the elixir is made from. Is the heist the moment something gets published, leaked, or nailed to a door?

### The Echo Unheard
- Måneklang revealed this name at the False Stage. The bards there were part of Ne'leak Shadowsong's bardic cult.
- Nothing has followed up on this yet. Is there a moment in the heist — or its aftermath — where this resurfaces?
- What *is* the Echo Unheard, and when do you want the party to start asking the right questions about it?

---

## The Question Underneath Everything

The party are, knowingly or not, the heirs of Tre Illium's unfinished project — a lunar balance that no one has championed in generations.

- Do any of them know this yet?
- Is the vault heist the moment one of them gets a glimpse — a document, a symbol, a name they recognise — that suggests their involvement in this is not coincidental?
- What's the earliest possible moment you want them to feel like they are part of something *older* than the Shadowbringers vs. DOL fight?
