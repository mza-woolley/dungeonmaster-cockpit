# IDEA: The Shadowbringer Mole

**Concept:** L.E.'s reaction to recent events isn't "the PCs did this" - it's "the Shadowbringers struck, as I expected." He's been watching Silas's crew for a while via a source close to them, and the Lumina Lab raid (and the coming vault heist) looks to him like confirmation of what that source already warned him about. The party is dangerous to L.E. only by *association* with the Shadowbringers, not yet by name.

---

## What We Know So Far

- A dying DOL spy left a sealed letter addressed to *L.E.*: *"The Shadowbringers, my lord - I've been had. I've been found. I will die a worthy death. Hail the Order of the Sun!"* - found by the party during the "Missing Guest" quest at The Final Rest, **Session 5** (see [Lorewarden Emeritus](../NPCs/Villains/Order%20of%20the%20Sun/Lorewarden%20Emeritus.md)).
- This **predates** the Lumina Lab raid (Session 6/7) - L.E. had eyes near/inside the Shadowbringers before any of the party's recent activity.
- The spy was "found" and died for it - implying the Shadowbringers (or someone close to them) rooted out a leak around Session 5.
- Open question: is this spy a one-off, already dead and done - or was there a network, with this letter just the moment one node got burned?
- Open question: does L.E. know *how* the Session 5 spy was caught - and does that knowledge make him more cautious about how hard he leans on any remaining/replacement source (e.g. Rissa), for fear of losing that line too?

---

## Where They Could've Been Involved

- Anyone in Silas's orbit who's had eyes on the party since Solvetra Middle - Du'geshk, Corwin Duskwatch, Rissa, Edrin Pell, Callen Droth, Marvin Tallowroot are all current heist-prep contacts the party is interacting with right now.
- Could explain how L.E. already has Key 4 and DOL has maintained quiet awareness of the vault's vulnerabilities despite Shadowbringer planning "for years."
- If the spy is dead (per the letter), any *replacement* mole is an open thread - someone currently helping the party with heist prep could be reporting back.

---

## Leading Candidate: Rissa "Whisper" Varn

[Rissa Varn](../NPCs/Neutral/Solvetra/Solvetra%20Intermellum/Rissa%20%22Whisper%22%20Varn.md) - information broker, "to the highest bidder" by nature and trade. She's a strong fit:

- Her entire business model is selling information for gold, no loyalty attached. Selling the party intel on the vault's inside contact *and* selling the DOL intel on the Shadowbringers/party isn't a contradiction for her - it's just two separate transactions.
- She's a **separate node** from the dead Session 5 spy - meaning the network wasn't fully burned, just thinned. Answers the "one-off or network" open question: it's a network, and Rissa is a live thread.
- Per [[Solvetra Headguard Replacement]], the new Headguard isn't the mole's handler - Rissa's DOL contact is most plausibly L.E. directly (or an intermediary that traces back to him), keeping his reach personal and quiet.
- Irony angle: the party may be *paying Rissa right now* for heist intel, unaware she could be billing the DOL for the same conversation from the other side.

**Open questions:**
- Does Rissa's information to the DOL help or hinder the heist - does she sandbag what she tells the DOL (still gets paid, doesn't burn her relationship with the party), or does she sell them something genuinely useful?
- Does anything about Session 7 give the party a reason to suspect her - or does this stay hidden until well after the heist?
- If exposed later, does Rissa flee, double down, or pivot to working for the party exclusively (highest bidder logic - the party could simply outbid the DOL)?
