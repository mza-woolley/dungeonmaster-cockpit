# World Reactions to Solvetra - Framework
_The party dismantled the Lumina Lab, killed the Headguard, and are about to rob the vault. The world noticed. Answer these questions to decide how._

---

## The DOL's Internal Response

- Valerius' letter said "I've already organised your replacement." Who is the replacement, and how are they different from Valerius? (Harder? Smarter? More political? More violent?)
- Does the DOL know it was the party specifically, or just that the Lab was destroyed?
- How does the DOL explain the Lab's destruction to the public in Solvetra? (Covered up? Blamed on criminals? Spun as a Shadowbringer attack?)
- Is there a DOL escalation - more guards, a lockdown, a purge of suspected Shadowbringers - triggered by what happened?
- Who inside the DOL is angry about Valerius dying? Who is quietly relieved?

---

## The Shadowbringers

- How do other Shadowbringer cells (outside Solvetra) hear about what the party did?
- Does it raise morale, or does it make them nervous that the party is too loud?
- Is there a cell that tries to make contact with the party directly - and should that be trusted?
- Silas is "planning something in Ars Lapis" - does word of Solvetra change his timeline or his ask?

---

- What's the most distorted version of events that's now being repeated as fact?
- Is there a rumour that accidentally helps the party - gives them cover, reputation, or an opening?
- Is there a rumour that could get them killed - mistaken identity, inflated bounty, wrong allegiance assumed?

---

## The Northern Star of the Primordial Fist

They sent goons to kill Fenrik at Solvetra Meadows. Those goons are dead. The party hasn't followed up on this yet.

- Does the NSPF know where the party is now?
- Who is Aurevon Beitomy Erstua - what do they look like, what do they want with Fenrik specifically, and how personally are they taking this?
- Is the NSPF operating independently, or are they a DOL tool? (The docs say they're DOL-funded - do they know they're being used, or do they think they're autonomous?)
- What's their next move - another ambush, a different approach, sending someone the party might actually trust?
- Does Silas know about them? Does he consider them a separate problem or part of the same war?

---

## Citizens & Ordinary People

- In Solvetra - how do citizens feel about the vault heist once it happens? Relief? Fear of DOL retaliation? Quiet celebration?
- Are there people in Solvetra who benefited from the DOL's control and will actively oppose the party now?
- Outside Solvetra - do ordinary people in other cities even know Solvetra is under DOL control, or does it look like business as usual from the outside?
- Is there a person - a merchant, a refugee, a former DOL acolyte - who carries news of Solvetra to Ars Lapis and the party can encounter them?

---

## Factions With Their Own Agenda

These aren't DOL or Shadowbringer. They exist in the world and have been watching.

- Is there a thieves' guild, a mercenary company, or a trade bloc that sees opportunity in Solvetra's instability?
- Is there a religious order (not DOL) that tracks what's happening - maybe one that remembers the lunar balance and sees the Tre Illium threads re-emerging?
- Is there a foreign power - another city-state, a kingdom, a dragon - that has an interest in who controls Solvetra?

---

## What the World Doesn't Know Yet

Things that are true but haven't surfaced publicly. Useful for drip-feeding later.

- Nobody outside a handful of people knows the Lumina Elixir is made from Worg abuse. When this gets out - how does it land?
- Nobody knows Valerius's replacement has already been chosen. Who is positioned to find out first?
- The distribution map pointed to Ars Lapis. Does Ars Lapis know it's a target?
