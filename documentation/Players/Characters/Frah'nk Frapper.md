# Frah'nk Frapper

**Age:** 33  
**Height:** 6'3"  
**Race:** Orc  
**Class:** Paladin  
**Background:** Soldier  
**Languages:** Common, Orc  
**Origin:** Wealthy hedge knight family — Order of Light  
**Affiliation:** Shadowbringers  
**Introduction:** Friend of Du'geshk Maharjuuj; sent to find the party by Silvanus Shadowfoot

---

## Background

Frah'nk was born into a hedge knight family with nominal ties to the Order of Light — comfortable, respected, but never truly powerful. His mother died in childbirth. His father raised him with the Order's values: duty, hierarchy, obedience. Frank never took to it. He could follow a rule he understood, but not one that existed simply because it always had.

His father was killed in battle when Frank was around 17. Shortly after, Frank was conscripted into **The Greyveil Suppression** — the OOTS-ordered clearing of what is now Solvetra East. At the time it was a thriving common district: open markets, mixed population, community-run. The residents were unarmed. Frank and the other conscripted Dawnbringers were told it was civic restructuring. They were sent in first. What followed was a forced removal at swordpoint — civilians, not combatants. The Order's proper forces watched from behind. Frank survived. The district didn't.

It was not a battle. There was no enemy. The Order called it progress. Frank called it what it was, walked away from his estate, his title, and everything he couldn't carry, and took his father's money and disappeared.

It was here that he met **Silvanus Shadowfoot** — also conscripted, also from a hedge knight family, also watching the same thing with the same expression. The friendship that formed that day has lasted since.

---

## The Sellsword Years

For the better part of a decade Frank worked as a mercenary — taking contracts across different cities, cultures, and factions. He was never picky about the job as long as it wasn't pointless, and he made a point of working with people the Order looked down on. Half-orcs, goblins, outcasts. He learned languages, customs, and perspectives that the hedge knight world had no interest in.

The more he saw of the world, the worse the Order looked. He witnessed a second DOL action — this one quieter, no battle, just enforcement. A town that had been openly trading lunar artefacts was visited by Order envoys. Within a fortnight, the market was gone, three people had vanished, and the rest had that familiar blank look Frank would later learn to associate with the Lumina Elixir. No resistance. No noise. Just erasure.

That was when he stopped being indifferent and started being angry.

---

## The Shadowbringers & Magic

Frank found the Shadowbringers through a contact in a city between jobs — or, more accurately, they found him. His reputation for taking the dirty work nobody else wanted had preceded him.

It was within the Shadowbringers that Frank first encountered runic magic — a pre-DOL form of spellworking that the Order had systematically suppressed and mislabelled as blasphemous. Where conventional magic flows through study and structure, Frank's magic is worked through marks — carved, drawn, burned. It doesn't look like the magic the Division teaches. It looks older, rougher, and wrong to anyone trained in the Order's doctrine. Frank took to it instinctively, possibly because it operates on a logic he can actually understand: cause, mark, effect. No authority required.

---

## Silvanus Shadowfoot

Frank's closest bond within the Shadowbringers is with **Silvanus Shadowfoot** — Silas' father, the former Oathbreaker Paladin who lost his leg, his wife, and his daughter to a thieving ambush and now walks the Path of the Shadowbringer. Two men shaped by what the Order did and didn't do for their families. The friendship is low-key and unspoken in the way old grievances tend to be — they don't need to explain themselves to each other.

It was Silvanus who recently sent Frank a letter. The message was simple: a group of adventurers had found their way into Silas' orbit, caused the kind of trouble that gets noticed, and Silvanus wanted someone he trusted to meet them. Not babysit — assess. Make sure they're the real thing and not a liability that'll get his son killed.

Frank also knows **Du'geshk Maharjuuj** from overlapping Shadowbringer operations — they've worked jobs together and have a mutual, no-nonsense respect.

---

## Personality

Frank has very little patience for rules that exist for their own sake and no patience at all for institutions that protect themselves at the expense of the people inside them. He doesn't hate the Order because he was taught to — he hates it because he watched it operate and drew his own conclusions.

He takes the hard roles because someone has to and because he's good at it. He doesn't perform sacrifice; he just doesn't see the point in skipping it.

He is not warm in the conventional sense but is reliably honest, which in this world counts for quite a lot.
