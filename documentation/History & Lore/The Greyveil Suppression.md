# The Greyveil Suppression

**Type:** Historical Event  
**Perpetrator:** Order of the Sun (OOTS) - Division of Lumination  
**Location:** Solvetra East (formerly known as Greyveil)  
**Status:** Buried - not part of official record

---

## Overview

The Greyveil Suppression was the forced clearing of Solvetra East, then a thriving common district known as Greyveil. Under the guise of civic restructuring, the Order of the Sun conscripted Dawnbringers and hedge knight families to remove the district's residents at swordpoint. The civilians were unarmed. The OOTS held back and let the conscripts go in first.

The district was subsequently renamed, rebuilt, and reoriented around Order-aligned institutions. The history of what it was - and what was done to take it - has been systematically removed from public record.

---

## What Was Lost

Greyveil was a mixed, community-run district. Open markets, shared institutions, a population that included races and cultures the Order considered undesirable. Much of what the party now sees in Solvetra East - the sanitised buildings, the Order's institutional presence, the purged library shelves - is the replacement layer built over what was erased.

---

## Known Participants

- **Silvanus Shadowfoot** - conscripted Dawnbringer. Followed orders. Has never forgiven himself. The Suppression was the first fracture in his faith; the later ambush on his family completed it. See [Silvanus Shadowfoot](../NPCs/Allies/Silvanus%20Shadowfoot.md).
- **Frah'nk Frapper** - conscripted alongside Silvanus. The two met during the Suppression. It was the defining event that drove Frah'nk away from the Order entirely. See [Frah'nk Frapper](../Players/Characters/Frah'nk%20Frapper.md).

---

## Current State

The name Greyveil does not appear in any official Solvetra record. Citizens alive today either do not know the history or have had it papered over. The Memory Archivist's Vault in Solvetra Intermellum may hold something - and the purged stacks of the Index of Absence in Solvetra East almost certainly did before the Order cleared them.
