# Tre Illium

**Type:** Historical — Founding Pact / Three-Part Alliance  
**Status:** Collapsed — principals dormant, unlocated, or silent  
**Era:** Pre-Division of Lumination

---

## Overview

Tre Illium was a three-part alliance formed to establish a permanent lunar presence across Le'Choix — a symbiosis of light and dark that no single faction could dominate. Their vehicle for this was **The Lunar Accord**.

---

## The Three Principals

| Role | Principal | Status |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Vassal of Light | Sibling Thorne | Believed dormant |
| Keeper of Shadow | Kaela Shadowsong | Believed dormant or unlocated |
| Dragon Regent | Myxtiferious | Silent — went unanswered at collapse |

---

## The Lunar Accord

The founding agreement binding the three principals to their roles:

- The **Vassal of Light** carries the flame without consuming what it touches.
- The **Keeper of Shadow** holds the gate without being claimed by what lies beyond it.
- The **Dragon Regent** stands between — sovereign over the balance, neither of the light nor the dark.

The Accord's goal: channel Shadowrealm magic through solar energy via the **Orb**, creating a divine moon presence — permanent harmony across Le'Choix. Without all three principals active, the Accord is non-viable.

**The Accord did not break — it went dormant.** It is technically still intact. The Dragon Regent's silence is what stalled it; the Accord could, in theory, be resumed.

---

## Collapse

When Myxtiferious stopped answering, Tre Illium lost its stabilising third. The other two principals pulled apart. The power vacuum allowed the Division of Lumination to rise as the only organised opposition — positioning light as the sole legitimate power and suppressing any path back to balance.

The DOL is actively invested in keeping the three principals separated. The Accord's dormancy is, to them, a strategic advantage.
